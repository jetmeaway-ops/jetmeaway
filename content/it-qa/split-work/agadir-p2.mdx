## How Agadir Compares to the Rest of Morocco

Agadir is Morocco's beach face — a modern, sun-and-sand resort rather than a historic medina town. Where [Marrakech](/blog/best-hotels-marrakech-2026) sells you the courtyard-riad fantasy and the souks, and [Fez](/blog/best-hotels-fez-2026) sells you the world's greatest medieval medina, Agadir sells you a six-kilometre Blue Flag bay, reliable winter sun, golf and a straightforward family beach holiday. It has no ancient old town — honest about that — but it is the one Moroccan destination built for lazing on the sand, and the best base for the Atlantic draws of Paradise Valley and the Taghazout surf.

The smart move for many UK travellers is to combine the two Moroccos: a few days on the Agadir coast for the beach, the surf and the winter sun, then the ~2.5-hour drive up to a [Marrakech](/blog/best-hotels-marrakech-2026) riad for the medina and the souks. Agadir gives you the sunshine and the sea; the imperial cities give you the history and the atmosphere. Together they make a well-rounded Morocco trip — and Agadir's £23 floor keeps the beach leg genuinely affordable.
