## The Scout's Take: Beachfront, One Block Back, or the Surf Coast?

Agadir has three practical bases, and the choice shapes both your budget and your holiday.

The **beachfront resort strip** — the hotels and aparthotels lining the bay between the marina and the promenade — is the classic choice: step out onto the sand, the promenade restaurants and the beach clubs, with the five-star seafront resorts here. It is the priciest ground, but the convenience is real.

**One or two blocks back** from the seafront is where the value lives. You get the same sunshine, the same short walk to the beach and the Souk El Had, and noticeably lower prices — this is where central Agadir's cheap aparthotels and 3★ hotels cluster. For most budget travellers, this is the smart base.

The **surf coast north of town** — Taghazout, Tamraght and Aourir, 15–25 minutes up the road — is the choice for surfers and a younger, laid-back crowd: point breaks, surf camps, yoga and boho cafés instead of resort pools. Several of the cheapest stays in this guide sit here.
