## Cheap Hotels in Montreal — 49 Real, Bookable Options From £65

Thirty-one properties, from ~£65 to ~£203 a night, listed cheapest first. **Read the heading literally: these are the most affordable rooms in an expensive sample, not cheap rooms.** In most cities a section like this would be the bargain tier. In late-August Montreal, the median entry below costs about £153 a night, only four properties come in below £100, and the top of the tier bumps straight into four-star chain pricing. If you have arrived here looking for a £40 room in a walkable European city break, Montreal in summer is not going to give you one at any star rating.

Three honest structural notes before you scan, because they determine whether a low number below is actually good news.

**First, the cheapest entries are cheapest because of where they are.** A large block of the sub-£150 list sits in the **airport belt around Dorval and Saint-Laurent**, roughly 20km west of downtown, selling to crews and connecting passengers rather than to sightseers. Another cluster sits in the **eastern boroughs** along Sherbrooke Est towards Mercier and Hochelaga-Maisonneuve. The east end is a defensible budget base because the green line metro runs through it and you are 20 to 30 minutes from the centre. The airport zone is not — treat those hotels as flight-night rooms and nothing else.

**Second, review depth varies enormously here.** Some entries below carry several thousand reviews, one carries twenty-two, and we have said so for each. A thin review base is a real risk you should price in, not a detail to skim.

**Third, star ratings in this tier are supplier-declared**, not a formal grading, so read a one or two-star entry as "basic but real". Two entries carry no star rating at all because they are loft and apartment stays rather than hotels. *From-prices are live rates sampled for late August and will be lower on off-peak dates.*
