## Cheap Hotels in Puerto Vallarta — 49 Real, Bookable Options From £29

This is the part of the guide that matters most, and Puerto Vallarta rewards it better than almost anywhere else in Mexico. **29 of the 49 hotels here are budget properties**, nearly all of them in **Centro**, the **Zona Romántica** or the streets around the **Río Cuale** — which is to say, in the best part of town. That is the crucial difference between here and the Caribbean coast: cheap in Puerto Vallarta does not mean far away. The £29 to £55 rooms below are a walk from the Malecón, the pier, the market and several hundred restaurants where dinner for two costs less than a round of drinks at a resort bar.

The honest trades are these. Budget hotels in the old town are usually small, often family-run, frequently up a flight of cobbled steps, and rarely soundproofed — Puerto Vallarta's centre is lively at night and a room facing the street will tell you so. Air conditioning is normal but not universal at the very bottom of the range, so check the room description in the humid summer months. And the review base varies wildly: some of these places have a thousand reviews, others twenty. We have flagged both. Every property below is real, distinct and currently bookable, listed cheapest first.
