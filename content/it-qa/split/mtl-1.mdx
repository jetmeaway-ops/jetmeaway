It is an exceptional hotel. It is also peak-August pricing on an exceptional hotel — check the same room in February before you decide.

**8. [The Ritz-Carlton, Montreal](/hotels/la_lp22ad7?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 5★ · 305 reviews · from ~£599/night. The second most expensive room here, and the most storied: this was the first hotel in North America to carry the Ritz-Carlton name, opened on Sherbrooke Street in 1912, and it is where Elizabeth Taylor and Richard Burton were married in 1964. A long closure and a very expensive restoration in the early 2010s brought it back with a glass extension, a courtyard garden and a pool. Old-money Montreal in physical form. Like the Four Seasons, the August rate is the August rate — the shoulder season is a different conversation.

### Mid-range — the four-star tier

Ten four-star hotels between roughly £132 and £319 — and yes, that top figure overlaps the five-star tier, which is the clearest single illustration of what late August does to Montreal pricing. Read this tier carefully, because it contains both the best value in the guide and its strangest quirk. The best value sits with **Hotel Montreal Metropolitan** and **Hotel Ruby Foo's**, both around £132 to £133, which undercuts most of the so-called budget tier below. The quirk is **M Montreal Hostel** at £319 a night, a number that plainly is not a dorm bed — see its entry for the explanation.

![Hotel Bonaventure Montreal — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/149128890.jpg)

**9. [Hotel Bonaventure Montreal](/hotels/la_lp21ff5?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 5,934 reviews · from ~£310/night. The most reviewed hotel in the entire guide and the most Montreal thing in it: a 1960s hotel perched on top of the Place Bonaventure exhibition complex, with a two-and-a-half acre rooftop garden and a **heated outdoor pool that stays open all winter**, so you can swim outside in a snowstorm with steam coming off the water. Rooms open onto the garden level rather than a corridor with a view of nothing. It plugs straight into Bonaventure metro and the Underground City. The rate is high for a four-star, and the pool is a genuine reason people pay it.

![Hotel Ruby Foo's — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/495086935.jpg)

**10. [Hotel Ruby Foo's](/hotels/la_lp2c28b?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 5,076 reviews · from ~£133/night. Five thousand reviews and £133 a night in peak August makes this one of the two strongest value picks in the whole guide. The catch, and it is a real one, is location: Ruby Foo's sits on the Décarie corridor in Côte-des-Neiges, north-west of downtown near Namur metro, which is a fifteen-minute train ride from the centre rather than a walk. In exchange you get a large, long-established hotel with an outdoor pool and free parking, at a price nothing downtown can match in summer. Take it if you are happy to commute.

![Hôtel Le Cantlie Suites — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/242636087.jpg)

**11. [Hôtel Le Cantlie Suites](/hotels/la_lp1df29?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 4,572 reviews · from ~£228/night. An all-suite tower on Sherbrooke Street West in the heart of the Golden Square Mile, a couple of minutes from Peel metro and the Museum of Fine Arts. Every unit is a suite with a separate sitting area and a kitchenette, and there is a rooftop pool with a view straight down the mountain slope. That kitchenette is the point for a family — two adults and two children eating breakfast in the room instead of a downtown restaurant recoups a chunk of the nightly rate. Very well reviewed and consistently so.

![Best Western Plus Hotel Montreal — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/609093398.jpg)

**12. [Best Western Plus Hotel Montreal](/hotels/la_lp99759?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 4,390 reviews · from ~£175/night. The dependable middle of the market: a large Best Western Plus with over four thousand reviews at £175, which in a late-August Montreal is a reasonable rather than remarkable number. Best Western Plus membership means an inspection regime and a complaints route, which is worth something. Rooms are plain and functional and nobody will describe the building as beautiful, but the rate holds up against far less proven independents at similar money. Check the exact address on the live page, as the location is the deciding factor here.

![Hilton Garden Inn Montreal Centre-Ville — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/474362341.jpg)

**13. [Hilton Garden Inn Montreal Centre-Ville](/hotels/la_lp41741?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 4,171 reviews · from ~£232/night. Exactly what a Hilton Garden Inn is everywhere on earth — clean, roomy, predictable, with a decent gym, a cooked breakfast option and a bar that stays open late enough — and here it is planted in central Ville-Marie within walking distance of the Quartier des Spectacles and the downtown metro stations. You are paying a location premium in August rather than for anything exotic. A safe booking if you want zero surprises and Hilton Honors points.

![Holiday Inn Hotel & Suites - Montreal Centre-ville Ouest by IHG — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/234145407.jpg)

**14. [Holiday Inn Hotel & Suites - Montreal Centre-ville Ouest by IHG](/hotels/la_lpa0ded?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 3,770 reviews · from ~£262/night. The west end of downtown, towards Guy-Concordia and the Shaughnessy Village side, which puts you close to Concordia University, the Faubourg and the Atwater end of Sainte-Catherine. Suites as well as rooms, an indoor pool, and the IHG reliability that makes a family booking easy to sign off. It is expensive for a Holiday Inn, and that is the August tax rather than the hotel — the same property on a November search is a different proposition.

![M Montreal Hostel — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/149269541.jpg)

**15. [M Montreal Hostel](/hotels/la_lp5a94a?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 3,179 reviews · from ~£319/night. Read that price twice, because it needs explaining. M Montreal is a well-known and genuinely good hostel in the Quartier Latin near Berri-UQAM, with a rooftop terrace, a hot tub and a bar, and it is normally one of the cheapest beds in the city. **A £319 lead-in rate on peak-August dates almost certainly reflects a private room or a whole-unit booking rather than a dorm bed**, and it tells you plainly how tight availability is in that week. Put your own dates in before you draw any conclusion from that figure — on almost any other week it will look completely different.

![Hotel Montreal Metropolitan, an Ascend Collection Hotel — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/233891394.jpg)

**16. [Hotel Montreal Metropolitan, an Ascend Collection Hotel](/hotels/la_lp1c0684?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 2,824 reviews · from ~£132/night. **The best value in the entire guide.** A four-star downtown hotel with nearly three thousand reviews at £132 a night in peak week is genuinely unusual, and it undercuts twenty-three of the thirty-one properties in the affordable tier below while being closer in and better rated than most of them. Ascend is Choice Hotels' independent-collection badge, so expect a boutique-ish room rather than a chain template. If one hotel in this post is worth booking on the spot, it is this one.

![Hotel Monville — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/135781550.jpg)

**17. [Hotel Monville](/hotels/la_lp9849f?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 2,685 reviews · from ~£203/night. The sharpest-looking mid-range room downtown: a purpose-built design hotel from the late 2010s on rue de Bleury, minutes from Place des Arts, the Palais des congrès and the Quartier des Spectacles, run on self-service check-in kiosks and — famously — a robot that delivers room-service orders to your door. Rooms are compact, black-and-glass, floor-to-ceiling windows, no clutter. It suits a couple doing a city break far better than a family. For festival-week proximity at £203, it is well judged.

![Hotel Universel Montréal — Montreal, Canada](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/95720840.jpg)

**18. [Hotel Universel Montréal](/hotels/la_lp351ae?city=Montreal)** — Montréal · 4★ · 2,591 reviews · from ~£165/night. The family pick of the tier 👪. It sits out east on Sherbrooke Est near the Olympic Park, so you are a short walk or a stop from the Biodôme, the Botanical Garden, the Insectarium and the Planetarium — the whole Space for Life cluster that keeps children occupied for two full days — with Viau and Pie-IX metro giving you a direct green line run downtown in about twenty minutes. Indoor pool, larger rooms, free parking, £165. For a family this beats most of what downtown offers at the price.
