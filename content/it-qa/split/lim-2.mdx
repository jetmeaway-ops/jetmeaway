## Cheap Hotels in Lima — 49 Real, Bookable Options From £18

This is the part of the guide that matters most, and Lima delivers on it better than almost any capital we have written up. **29 of the 49 hotels here are budget properties**, running from **£18** to **£44** a night — a budget tier with a ceiling lower than a single night in a Travelodge on the M6. And the crucial detail is where they are: this is not a list of rooms exiled to the outskirts. Five internationally branded **ibis** hotels sit in **Miraflores** and **San Isidro** between £30 and £38. Two Casa Andina hotels sit in Miraflores at £38 and £39. A **four-star Novotel** appears at £42 and a four-star **Suites del Bosque** at £43.

The honest trade at the very bottom of the range is evidence rather than location. The cheapest rooms here — the £18 airport property, the £25 boutique hostal, the £29 pair — carry review counts in the double figures rather than the thousands, which means less certainty about what you are walking into. The sweet spot, and the answer for most readers, is the £30 to £40 band, where you get four-figure review counts, international branding and a Miraflores or San Isidro postcode. Every property below is real, distinct and currently bookable, listed cheapest first.
