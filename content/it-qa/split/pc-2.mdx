## Cheap Hotels in Punta Cana — 40 Real, Bookable Options From £22

This is the part of the guide that matters most, and it is where Punta Cana's honest reality lives. **20 of the 40 hotels here are budget properties**, and — this is the bit nobody markets — **almost none of them are gated beachfront all-inclusives**. They are room-only hotels, guesthouses and self-catering aparthotels, most of them in or beside the small local beach town of **El Cortecito and Los Corales** in Bávaro, with a few a little inland. That is not a catch, it is how Punta Cana works: the beachfront strip was built as large all-inclusive resorts, so the cheap, independent market clusters in the one pocket that has a public beach, a strip of open-air restaurants and small shops. So the trade is plain. You are booking a bed rather than a full-board resort, you will eat in the town's restaurants or self-cater, and some stays are a short walk to the public beach while others are a taxi ride inland. In exchange you get a real room from £22 a night on the same coconut-palm coast, local prices for food and drink, and a week in Punta Cana for less than a couple of nights inside a resort. Every property below is real, distinct and currently bookable, listed cheapest first.
