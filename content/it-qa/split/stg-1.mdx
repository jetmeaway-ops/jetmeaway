![Hôtel de l'Europe by HappyCulture — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/286182036.jpg)

**7. [Hôtel de l'Europe by HappyCulture](/hotels/la_lp2b9bc?city=Strasbourg)** — Grande Île, near the cathedral · 4★ · 5,007 reviews · from ~£79/night. The best value in the four-star tier and one of the best in the guide: a HappyCulture four-star in a historic building on the Grande Île, a few minutes from the cathedral, with more than five thousand reviews — at around £79, cheaper than several of the budget-tier hotels below. If you want a central, well-reviewed, properly four-star old-town base at the lowest sensible price, start here. It is the clearest "punches above its rate" pick among the mid-range hotels.

![Hôtel Du Dragon — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/621208566.jpg)

**8. [Hôtel Du Dragon](/hotels/la_lp650b1?city=Strasbourg)** — Krutenau, south of the Grande Île · 4★ · 3,918 reviews · from ~£108/night. A well-regarded independent four-star just south of the island in the Krutenau quarter, near the river and an easy walk over a bridge into the old town. It trades on quiet, contemporary rooms set around a courtyard and a location that is central without being in the middle of the market-square noise — a good choice for light sleepers who still want to walk everywhere. Nearly four thousand reviews and a rate around £108.

![LIFE RENAISSANCE - New Concept - Place Kléber — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/191848118.jpg)

**9. [LIFE RENAISSANCE - New Concept - Place Kléber](/hotels/la_lp6565e6c6?city=Strasbourg)** — Place Kléber · 4★ · 3,445 reviews · from ~£207/night. The most expensive hotel in this entire guide, and — the honest quirk worth flagging — a four-star, not a five-star. What you are paying for is the address: a "new concept" design property directly on **Place Kléber**, the grand central square of Strasbourg, which is about as prime as a city-centre location gets. The rate of around £207 reflects that square-front position and the contemporary fit-out rather than five-star ceremony. Book it if being on Kléber itself matters to you; there are better-value four-stars a two-minute walk away.

![Hôtel Régent Contades By Stay Collection — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/160884650.jpg)

**10. [Hôtel Régent Contades By Stay Collection](/hotels/la_lp20c8c?city=Strasbourg)** — near Contades park / Neustadt · 4★ · 3,249 reviews · from ~£104/night. A refined four-star on the quays near the Contades park, on the edge of the grand German-era Neustadt district and a short walk from both the old town and the start of the European quarter. It is a quieter, more residential base than the island hotels, with a classic feel and river views from some rooms, and it sits under the same Stay Collection umbrella as the REGENT Petite France. Around £104, with more than three thousand reviews.

![Hilton Strasbourg — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/514108515.jpg)

**11. [Hilton Strasbourg](/hotels/la_lp1ba0a?city=Strasbourg)** — Wacken, near the convention centre and European district · 4★ · 3,244 reviews · from ~£97/night. The big international-brand four-star, out by the Wacken convention centre and the European institutions north of the centre — the natural pick for anyone in town for a conference or official Parliament business, with full Hilton facilities, leisure amenities and Hilton Honors points. It is a tram ride from the cathedral rather than a walk, so for a pure leisure break the old-town hotels are better placed, but for the institutions and events it is exactly where you want to be, at around £97.

![Hôtel Diana Dauphine — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/621204728.jpg)

**12. [Hôtel Diana Dauphine](/hotels/la_lp45ecd?city=Strasbourg)** — between the station and the Grande Île · 4★ · 3,229 reviews · from ~£87/night. A comfortable, well-run four-star in the streets between Gare de Strasbourg and the old town — a ten-minute walk to the cathedral in one direction and to the trains for Colmar and Paris in the other. That in-between position is genuinely useful: cheaper than the island, still central, and ideal for a trip that mixes the city with day trips into Alsace. More than three thousand reviews and a rate around £87 make it one of the tier's dependable value picks.

![Le Jean-Sébastien Bach — Strasbourg, France](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/586450140.jpg)

**13. [Le Jean-Sébastien Bach](/hotels/la_lp2a167?city=Strasbourg)** — central · 4★ · 2,946 reviews · from ~£45/night. The value shock of the guide: a four-star at around £45 a night, cheaper than most of the budget tier below and backed by nearly three thousand reviews. Rates like this are lead-in prices on quiet, flexible dates rather than a promise for every night — and they will not survive December or a Parliament week — but on the right dates this is the single best price-to-star ratio in the whole post. Worth checking first and comparing against your dates before anything else.
