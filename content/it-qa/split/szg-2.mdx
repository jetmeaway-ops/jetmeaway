Thirty-five properties, from about £124 to £189 a night, listed cheapest first. **Read the heading literally: these are the most affordable rooms in an expensive, festival-week sample, not cheap rooms.** In most cities a section like this would be the bargain tier. In late-August Salzburg the median entry below costs about £157 a night, not a single one comes in below £120, and the top of the tier bumps straight into four-star chain pricing. If you have arrived here hoping for a £50 room in a walkable European city break, Salzburg in festival week is not going to give you one at any star rating.

Three honest structural notes before you scan, because they decide whether a low number below is actually good news.

**First, the cheapest entries are cheapest because of where they are.** Almost nothing in this tier is inside the historic centre. The rooms cluster instead around the **main railway station** to the north, along the **outer arterial roads** north and south, and out by the **airport** to the west. The station area is the one genuinely useful budget base for a visitor without a car — it is a 15 to 20 minute walk or a short bus into the old town, and it is where the Munich and Vienna trains arrive. The outer-road and airport hotels are cheaper again but assume you will bus or drive in each day, which for a two-day city break gets old fast. We have flagged the trade hotel by hotel.

**Second, review depth varies enormously here.** Some entries below carry many thousands of reviews — one has over fifteen thousand — and one carries just sixty-one. A thin review base is a real risk you should price in, not a detail to skim, and we have said so for each.

**Third, star ratings in this tier are supplier-declared**, not a formal grading, so read a two-star entry as "basic but real" rather than as a graded classification. Several of the strongest entries carry **no star rating at all** — including two of the best-reviewed hotels in the whole post, COOL MAMA and Am Neutor — because they are modern budget-design, apartment or pension stays that have not sought a classification. Unrated here does not mean bad; it means unclassified. *From-prices are live rates sampled for late August and will be lower on off-peak dates.*
