## Cheap Hotels in Graz — 49 Real, Bookable Options From £40

Thirty-eight properties, from about **£40 to £115 a night**, listed cheapest first — and this is where Graz really earns its reputation as one of the best-value city breaks in Austria. Unlike Salzburg or Vienna, where the cheapest rooms start well above £100, Graz gives you a real budget floor: the a&o by the station opens the tier at **£40**, and there is a deep spread of chain budget hotels, apartments, hostels and characterful family-run houses through the £50 to £115 band. The prices are moderate rather than rock-bottom — this is a prosperous UNESCO city, not a backpacker bargain bin — but pound for pound it is excellent.

Three honest structural notes before you scan, because they decide whether a low number below is actually good news.

**First, the cheapest entries are cheapest because of where they are.** The budget floor clusters around the **Hauptbahnhof** to the west and out along the tram lines — towards the **Messe** exhibition grounds in the south-east, the suburb of **Hart bei Graz** to the east, and the outer ring generally. The station area is the one genuinely useful budget base for a visitor without a car: it is a flat 15 to 20 minute walk or a few tram stops into the old town, and it is where the Vienna trains arrive. The outer hotels are cheaper again but assume you will ride the tram or drive in — though in calm, tram-friendly Graz that is a far smaller chore than the equivalent in a bigger city. We have flagged the trade hotel by hotel.

**Second, review depth varies enormously here.** Some entries below carry many thousands of reviews — one has over nine thousand — and a handful carry only a few dozen. A thin review base is a real risk you should price in, not a detail to skim, and we have said so for each. It matters most among the apartment and self-check-in stays at the cheaper end.

**Third, star ratings in this tier are supplier-declared**, not a formal grading, so read a two-star entry as "basic but real" rather than as a graded classification. Several of the strongest entries carry **no star rating at all** — including some of the best-reviewed hotels in the whole post, like the B&B Hotel Graz City-Süd and the B&B Hotel Graz-Hbf — because they are modern budget-chain, apartment or self-check-in stays that have not sought a classification. Unrated here does not mean bad; it means unclassified. *From-prices are live rates sampled for a shoulder-season stay and will move a little with your dates.*
