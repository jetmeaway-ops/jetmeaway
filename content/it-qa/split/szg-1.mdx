The last thing to fix in your head before the hotels is **the shape of the city**, because where you sleep matters here. Salzburg is small and split by the **Salzach river**. On the **left bank** is the **Altstadt**, the UNESCO-listed baroque old town under the fortress — beautiful, pedestrianised, and the most expensive and most limited place to stay. [Leonardo Hotel Salzburg Airport](/hotels/la_lp41713?city=Salzburg)** — Salzburg · 4★ · 6,089 reviews · from ~£99/night. **The cheapest room in this entire guide**, and the catch is in the name: it is by Salzburg Airport, west of the city, not in the centre. As an airport four-star it is excellent value — modern rooms, over six thousand reviews, easy for an early flight or a late arrival — but for a city break you would be busing in and out, with the 2 and 10 buses reaching the centre in about 20 minutes. Treat it as a flight-night hotel, or a budget-with-a-commute base for anyone happy to ride in each morning.

![IMLAUER HOTEL PITTER Salzburg — Salzburg, Austria](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/156509745.jpg)

**10. [IMLAUER HOTEL PITTER Salzburg](/hotels/la_lp20294?city=Salzburg)** — Salzburg · 4★ · 5,512 reviews · from ~£406/night. Read that price against the star rating, because £406 for a four-star is exactly what the festival does. The Pitter is a large, long-established hotel on the Neustadt side near Mirabell, best known for its **rooftop restaurant and bar with a fortress view**, and it carries over five thousand reviews. It is a genuinely good hotel with a landmark position — £406 is a festival-week rate on it, not a normal one. Put your own dates in before you judge it.

![Dorint City-Hotel Salzburg — Salzburg, Austria](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/482203645.jpg)

**11. [Dorint City-Hotel Salzburg](/hotels/la_lp1d16d?city=Salzburg)** — Salzburg · 4★ · 5,148 reviews · from ~£159/night. One of the better-value four-stars in the tier: a well-reviewed city hotel at £159, centrally placed near the river and the congress centre on the Neustadt side. Rooms are business-standard and reliable rather than characterful, which is precisely what a lot of people want from a walkable central base. With over five thousand reviews behind it, a sensible, low-drama booking.

![ARCOTEL Castellani Salzburg — Salzburg, Austria](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/585075415.jpg)

**12. [ARCOTEL Castellani Salzburg](/hotels/la_lp33b6b?city=Salzburg)** — Salzburg · 4★ · 4,878 reviews · from ~£138/night. The cheapest four-star in the mid tier that is not by the airport, at £138 with nearly five thousand reviews. It is a converted former monastery with a large garden, set a little out from the very centre — check the exact location and the walk or bus into the old town on the live page — which is why it prices below the more central four-stars. Genuinely good value if you do not mind being slightly out, with parking and grounds most central hotels cannot offer.

![IMLAUER Palais Mirabell — Salzburg, Austria](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/10034749.jpg)

**13. [IMLAUER Palais Mirabell](/hotels/la_lp1caf8?city=Salzburg)** — Salzburg · 4★ · 3,809 reviews · from ~£334/night. A smart four-star in a palais-style building near the Mirabell Gardens, from the same local IMLAUER group as the Pitter, with a rooftop terrace and a strong central position on the Neustadt side. At £334 it is festival-week pricing on a genuinely characterful, well-placed hotel — lovely, and priced squarely for the season. Check what it costs on a spring or autumn date and the picture changes.

![Altstadthotel Kasererbräu — Salzburg, Austria](https://static.cupid.travel/hotels/ex_fb07aa46_z.jpg)

**14. [Altstadthotel Kasererbräu](/hotels/la_lp37589?city=Salzburg)** — Salzburg · 4★ · 3,775 reviews · from ~£320/night. The one mid-tier hotel that is unambiguously **inside the Altstadt**, tucked just behind the cathedral in the pedestrian old town — which is exactly why it costs £320 in festival week. A traditional four-star with a small spa in the historic cellar, it puts you inside the postcard: you walk out of the door into the baroque core, with the fortress overhead. If being in the old town itself matters more to you than the price, this is the mid-tier way to do it.
