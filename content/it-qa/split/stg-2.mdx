## Cheap Hotels in Strasbourg — 49 Real, Bookable Options From £35

Thirty-six budget properties, from about **£35 to £79** a night, listed cheapest first — the deepest tier in this guide by far, and the one most UK readers actually need. Strasbourg's cheap end is genuinely good, and it splits into two useful groups: **central budget hotels and aparthotels** around the station and the edges of the Grande Île, where you pay a little more for a walk to the cathedral, and **outer chains and aparthotels** around the Zénith arena, Lingolsheim, the port and the Rhine crossing, where rates drop and you rely on the tram. The tram is fast, frequent and cheap, so the outer options are a real saving rather than a compromise.

Three honest notes before you scan them. **Review counts vary enormously in this tier** — some of these hotels have thousands of reviews, some have a dozen, and we have said so for each, because a thin review base is a real risk to price in. **Star ratings at the very bottom are supplier-declared or non-graded** (the hostels, the novelty stays, the ultra-budget chains), so read a low or zero star count as "basic but real" rather than a formal grading. And most important: **these from-prices are for ordinary flexible dates**. They are not what you will pay for a December Christmas-market weekend or a Parliament session week, when the cheapest rooms sell out first and the rest reprice upward.
