## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Glasgow?** **easyJet and Ryanair** on headline price (from ~£25-£50 off-peak). **BA** runs frequent flights from Heathrow and London City.

**How long is the flight?** About **1 hour 15 minutes** from London — ~3.5-4.5 hours door-to-door, a little faster than the train.

**Which airport?** **Glasgow (GLA)** is 8 miles out and more convenient (easyJet, BA, Jet2); **Prestwick (PIK)** is 30 miles out (mainly Ryanair) — cheaper but a longer transfer.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Whichever is **nearest** — Glasgow flies from many UK cities.

**When should I book?** **3-6 weeks** ahead most months; **6-10 weeks** for summer, events and Christmas.

**Do I need a passport?** No — it's a **domestic** flight, but airlines require **photo ID**.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### easyJet & Ryanair — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £25-£60 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. easyJet flies to Glasgow (GLA) from many UK cities; Ryanair uses Prestwick (PIK) and Glasgow. Frequent flights, regular sales.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; hold bag and allocated seat cost extra. Check whether a Ryanair fare uses Prestwick.

### British Airways — frequency and the central airports

**Typical fare 2026:** £60-£120 return; "Basic" domestic fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** Frequent flights from Heathrow and London City to GLA, Avios earning, a bag on standard fares.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the budget carriers.

### Jet2 & Loganair

**Pros:** Jet2 flies GLA with baggage included from several bases; Loganair adds regional Scottish and UK connections.
**Cons:** Fewer London options.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, easyJet or Ryanair win on fare (check the airport). For a bag included or London City convenience, Jet2 or BA. Compare the all-in price and the airport.

## Fly or take the train to Glasgow?

For **London-Glasgow**, flying is usually a little faster door-to-door — about 3.5-4.5 hours versus 4.5-5 hours on the Avanti West Coast train from Euston to Glasgow Central. But a cheap flight's edge shrinks once you add airport transfers and hold-bag fees, and the train is city centre to city centre with a generous luggage allowance. An Avanti Advance ticket booked ahead can be competitive on cost.

**When flying wins:** most UK cities (no fast rail link), and London if you value the slightly quicker door-to-door time or find a cheap hand-luggage fare.
**When the train is worth it:** London travellers who prefer city-centre convenience, are carrying luggage, or find a cheap Advance fare.

From everywhere outside the West Coast Main Line, fly.

## Do you need ID or a passport?

Glasgow is in the UK, so a flight from elsewhere in Britain is **domestic** — no passport is legally required. But **every airline requires an accepted form of photo ID**, and each sets its own list. Most accept a valid passport, and some accept a photo driving licence on domestic routes. **Check your specific airline's accepted-ID list before travelling** and take an ID it accepts.

## Common mistakes UK-Glasgow travellers make

1. **Booking a Prestwick fare thinking it's Glasgow.** Prestwick is 30 miles out — factor the longer transfer.
2. **Not comparing the train from London.** The Avanti train is city-centre and can be competitive on cost — check it.
3. **Judging fares on the headline price.** Add airport transfers and a hold bag and a "cheap" flight can cost more.
4. **Travelling with no accepted photo ID.** It's domestic, but airlines require ID — take one they accept.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online, or travel hand-luggage only for a weekend.
6. **Ignoring event dates.** Concerts and matches spike hotels — check the calendar.
7. **Overlooking the Highlands.** Glasgow is a gateway to Loch Lomond and the West Highlands — plan a day out.
