## The Scout's Take: Moalboal, Badian or Cebu City?

**Moalboal** (about 3 hours south-west of the airport) is where most of Cebu's magic happens — Panagsama Beach's drop-off reef puts the **sardine run** and **green sea turtles** a short swim from shore, the diving is world-class, and it's the base for day trips to Kawasan Falls and Osmeña Peak. It's a barefoot, backpacker-friendly strip of dive shops, beach bars and cheap guesthouses.

**Badian**, a little further down the coast, is the launch town for **Kawasan Falls canyoneering** — the turquoise-pool jumping-and-sliding adventure Cebu is famous for. Sleeping here means an early start before the day-trip crowds arrive. It's the cheapest place to stay on the island, with guesthouses from £9.

**Cebu City** is the historic, urban heart — Magellan's Cross, the Basilica del Santo Niño, Fort San Pedro, the Taoist Temple and the big malls and transport links. Two nights here bookend a coast trip nicely. Rooms are clean, simple and cheap, from around £12.

For most first trips: two nights in Cebu City, then three or four on the south-west coast. For divers and adventurers: go straight to Moalboal and Badian. For the tightest budget: Badian and Moalboal beat the city on price.

## UK Practicalities

- **Getting there**: No direct UK flights — connect via Manila (MNL) or a Gulf/Asian hub (Doha, Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong), then on to Mactan-Cebu (CEB). [Search flights to CEB](/flights?to=CEB). Check the [UK government entry rules for the Philippines](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/philippines) before you fly.
- **Airport transfers**: Grab or metered taxi from Mactan-Cebu to the city is 30–45 minutes. Moalboal and Badian are a 2.5–3 hour drive or ceres bus from the South Bus Terminal, then a tricycle to Panagsama Beach.
- **When to visit**: Dry season November–May (peak December–February and Easter); wet/typhoon season June–October is cheaper, greener and quieter, with the odd washed-out day.
- **Fees**: No island-wide tourist tax, but Kawasan Falls has an environmental/entrance fee, canyoneering has a guide-and-gear fee, and Moalboal marine tours carry a small boat charge. Carry peso (PHP) cash for small towns.
- **Budget**: A Cebu week on the budget tier — £9–34 a night on rooms, cheap local food, ceres buses and tricycles — can land well under £500 per person before flights, canyoneering and whale-shark tours included.
