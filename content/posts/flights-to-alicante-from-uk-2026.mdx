## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & easyJet — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £17-£50 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** Ryanair runs around 209 flights a week on the route — one of its busiest Spanish destinations — with easyJet around 140. The lowest headline fares and a wide UK airport spread. Ideal for a hand-luggage break.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra — the fare climbs once you check a bag.

### Jet2 — the beach-holiday all-rounder

**Typical fare 2026:** £65-£130 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance.
**Pros:** Baggage often included, friendly leisure service, strong regional coverage — frequently the best all-in value for a family Costa Blanca holiday with checked bags.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the ultra-budget carriers; fewer London departures.

### Vueling — the extra option

**Typical fare 2026:** £45-£95 return; hold bag extra on cheapest fares.
**Pros:** Adds frequency and sometimes undercuts the others on specific dates.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares cabin-only; check baggage terms.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair or easyJet win on price. For a family beach holiday with checked bags, Jet2's all-in fare is often the best value. Compare Vueling too — it occasionally beats them all on a given date.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Nov):** Book **4-8 weeks out**.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug), Easter, school holidays:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — peak Costa Blanca demand, fares only rise.
- **Shoulder peaks (May-Jun, Sep):** Book **6-10 weeks out** for the best balance of beach weather and price.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

Set a price alert for your dates and book when a budget carrier drops a sale fare rather than paying the standing rate.

## Alicante Airport to the Costa Blanca resorts

Alicante-Elche Airport (ALC) is well connected to the coast. The **C-6 bus** links the airport to central Alicante, where the **TRAM light rail** continues up the coast towards Benidorm. Direct **coaches** run to Benidorm (about an hour) and towards Torrevieja to the south, and **pre-booked transfers and taxis** are widely available for all the resorts. For central Alicante the C-6 bus is cheapest; for Benidorm or Torrevieja, a direct coach or transfer is usually easiest. ## Common mistakes UK-Alicante travellers make

1. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** With ~75 flights a day from across the UK, your local airport is almost always cheapest.
2. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £30 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can be beaten by a Jet2 fare that includes baggage.
3. **Booking the summer peak late.** July-August is peak Costa Blanca — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
4. **Missing the May-June and September value window.** Great beach weather at below peak-summer prices.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Not planning the resort transfer.** Benidorm and Torrevieja need a coach or transfer — sort it before you fly.
