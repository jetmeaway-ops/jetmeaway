## Majorca All-Inclusive FAQs

### Which airport do I fly into for Majorca?

Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI) is the island's only airport and one of the busiest in Europe through summer, with direct flights from most UK airports. From PMI it's a short transfer — often 15–30 minutes — to Palma, Playa de Palma and the nearby bays. Search UK flights to PMI and compare live Majorca hotel prices to line up your dates.

### Are there adults-only all-inclusive hotels in Majorca?

Plenty. Palma and Playa de Palma have a strong adults-only scene — Hotel Be Live Adults Only Marivent, Be Live Adults Only La Cala, Ca n'Alexandre and Hotel JS Palma Stay all cater to couples and groups wanting a quieter stay. Many pair the calm with a spa. Filter toward the adults-only four-stars on this list, and confirm the concept on each hotel's live page.

### Which part of Majorca is best for an all-inclusive family holiday?

For classic family all-inclusive, Playa de Palma and the northern bay of Alcúdia lead — long sandy beaches, shallow water and big village-style resorts. Magaluf and Santa Ponsa in the west also cater heavily to families. Palma city itself suits couples and culture-focused travellers more than young families. Match the district to your trip and check each hotel's live page.

### Is Palma a good base for a Majorca holiday?

Palma is excellent if you want a city break with beach access rather than a pure resort holiday — a stunning old town, Gothic cathedral, marina and tapas scene, with Playa de Palma's beaches a short ride away. It leans toward four-star B&B, half-board and adults-only spa hotels. For a full all-inclusive family village, look to the bays instead.
