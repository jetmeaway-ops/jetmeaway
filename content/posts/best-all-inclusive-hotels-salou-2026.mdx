## Compare other Spanish holiday spots

Salou is the family-and-theme-park end of Spain's costas — long golden Costa Dorada beaches, PortAventura World next door, and a 15-minute REU transfer. If you're weighing it against the rest of the coast, its closest Costa Brava cousin is [Lloret de Mar](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-lloret-de-mar-2026), which leans livelier and younger, while [Benidorm](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-benidorm-2026) on the Costa Blanca is bigger, busier and cheaper year-round. Down on the Costa del Sol, [Marbella](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-marbella-2026) and [Málaga](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-malaga-2026) trade the theme-park focus for city culture and southern sun.

For guaranteed sunshine and volcanic-island scenery, compare the Canaries — [Tenerife](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-tenerife-2026), [Gran Canaria](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-gran-canaria-2026) and [Lanzarote](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-lanzarote-2026) — or the Balearic favourite [Majorca](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-majorca-2026). For the bigger picture, start with our [family holidays in Spain](/blog/family-holidays-spain) guide, and if you want to pair Salou with a couple of nights in the city, browse the [best hotels in Barcelona](/blog/best-hotels-barcelona-2026) just up the coast. For a family theme-park beach holiday with a short transfer, Salou is the Costa Dorada's standout pick.

## Salou All-Inclusive FAQs

### How far is Salou from Reus Airport (REU)?

Salou sits about 12–15 km from Reus Airport (REU), roughly a 15–20 minute drive — one of the shortest airport-to-resort transfers in Spain. Most hotels and tour operators arrange private or shared transfers, and taxis are quick and inexpensive. That short hop is a big part of Salou's appeal for UK families with young children. Barcelona (BCN) is the larger alternative, about a 60–75 minute transfer to the north.

### Which Salou hotels are best for PortAventura World?

Several hotels sit right beside PortAventura World and bundle park access into the rate — the PortAventura-branded properties (Hotel PortAventura, Colorado Creek, Lucy's Mansion) plus Ponient Pirámide and Ponient Dorada Palace include tickets or wristbands. These are the ones built for a theme-park trip. Tap any hotel for live rates and current park-access details on your dates.

### What does all-inclusive mean in Salou?

In Salou, all-inclusive typically covers buffet breakfast, lunch and dinner, plus local drinks — beer, wine, soft drinks and house spirits — through set hours, along with snacks and pool facilities. It's the standard Spanish costa model rather than Turkey's ultra board, so imported spirits and à la carte restaurants are often extras. Check each hotel's live page for its exact board before booking.

### Are there adults-only all-inclusive hotels in Salou?

Yes. Salou skews family-oriented, but there are adults-only and adults-recommended options — H10 Delfín, Magnolia Hotel Salou, Hotel SunClub CalaVinya and Hotel Salou Sunset are aimed at couples and quieter stays. Filter toward these if you want to avoid the big family complexes, and check each hotel's live page for its current adults policy before booking.

### How much does a Salou all-inclusive holiday cost for a UK family?

On our midweek searches, all-inclusive family rooms start from roughly £125 a night, with the mid-range four-stars around £160–£280 and PortAventura-bundled stays from about £200. Because meals and local drinks are covered, the nightly rate is close to your all-in spend — a big part of why Salou stays competitive against destinations with cheaper rooms but pay-as-you-go food.
