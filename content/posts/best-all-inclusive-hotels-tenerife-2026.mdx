For a city break to bolt on, compare [Barcelona hotels](/blog/best-hotels-barcelona-2026). For a warm beach every month of the year, though, Tenerife's south coast is hard to beat.

## Tenerife All-Inclusive FAQs

### Which is the best area in Tenerife for an all-inclusive holiday?

The sunny south is where UK all-inclusive holidays cluster — Costa Adeje for polished resorts and spas, Playa de las Américas for livelier nightlife, and Los Cristianos for a quieter, family-friendly feel. Costa Adeje is the upscale end, with Blue Flag beaches, Siam Park nearby and the shortest transfer from Tenerife South (TFS). This guide centres on that Costa Adeje south-coast resort strip.

### How far is Costa Adeje from Tenerife South Airport (TFS)?

Costa Adeje sits about 20 km west of Tenerife South Airport (TFS), roughly a 20–30 minute drive depending on your resort — one of the shortest airport-to-resort hops in the Canaries, and a big part of the south coast's appeal for UK families with young children.

### Is Tenerife warm all year round?

Yes — the south coast is one of Europe's few genuine year-round beach destinations, with daytime highs around 20–22°C in winter and 27–29°C in summer. The Costa Adeje strip sits in a rain-shadow microclimate that keeps it drier and sunnier than the greener north, which is why UK travellers book Tenerife in January as readily as July.

### Is Siam Park near Costa Adeje?

Yes — Siam Park, regularly rated one of the best water parks in the world, sits between Costa Adeje and Playa de las Américas, a short taxi or shuttle from most south-coast resorts. Aqualand in Costa Adeje itself is the other big option. Staying on the strip puts both minutes away.

### Are there budget all-inclusive hotels in Tenerife?

Yes — beyond the five-star resorts, the Costa Adeje strip has three-star aparthotels and smaller hotels from around £89 a night on our midweek searches. These suit self-catering families, couples on a budget, and travellers who spend their days at the beach or Siam Park. Some budget properties are room-only or half-board rather than full all-inclusive.
