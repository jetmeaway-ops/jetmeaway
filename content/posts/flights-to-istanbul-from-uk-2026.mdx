## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Istanbul?** **Pegasus and AJet** into Sabiha Gökçen (from ~£45-£90 off-peak). **Turkish Airlines** into the main IST is full-service with a bag included; **BA and easyJet** also fly.

**How long is the flight?** About **3 hours 45 to 3 hours 55 minutes** from London.

**Do I need a visa?** **No** — UK travellers are now **visa-free** for Turkey tourism (up to 90 days). Passport valid 150+ days from entry.

**IST or SAW?** **IST** (European side, Turkish Airlines/BA) is more convenient for the historic centre; **SAW** (Asian side, Pegasus/AJet) is often cheaper but a longer transfer.

**When should I book?** **6-10 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer, Easter and the shoulder peaks.

<a id="visa"></a>

## Good news: no visa needed for 2026

For years, UK travellers to Turkey had to buy an e-visa. That has changed: **UK passport holders no longer need a visa or e-visa to visit Turkey for tourism**, and can enter **visa-free for up to 90 days** in any 180-day period.

- Your **passport should be valid for at least 150 days** from your date of entry and have a blank page for the stamp.
- The visa-free rule covers **tourism and short business trips** — different rules apply for work or long stays.
- Entry requirements can change, so **confirm the current position** on the [UK government's Turkey travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/turkey) shortly before you travel.

## Istanbul Airport (IST) vs Sabiha Gökçen (SAW)

Istanbul has two big airports on opposite sides of the city, and which you fly into matters more here than in most cities.

| Airport | Side of city | Airlines | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Istanbul Airport (IST) | European side | Turkish Airlines, BA, Wizz | Convenience to the historic centre; metro + HAVAIST bus |
| Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) | Asian side | Pegasus, AJet | Cheaper fares; longer transfer to the European sights |

**The convenience call.** Most of Istanbul's headline sights — Sultanahmet, the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, the Grand Bazaar — are on the **European side**, closer to **IST**. **SAW**, on the Asian side, often has the cheapest fares (Pegasus and AJet), but the transfer across the city takes longer. For a short trip where time matters, IST is usually worth a slightly higher fare; for a budget trip where you don't mind the transfer, SAW can save money. Factor the transfer into your comparison.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Pegasus & AJet — the budget leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £45-£120 return, hand-luggage only into SAW.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. Pegasus flies from Gatwick and Stansted; AJet adds capacity. Good for a budget city break.
**Cons:** Into Sabiha Gökçen (longer transfer); cheapest fares are cabin-only with paid extras.

### Turkish Airlines — the flag carrier

**Typical fare 2026:** £120-£250 return; standard fares include a generous bag.
**Pros:** Flies into the main IST (its hub) with excellent onboard service, a generous checked-baggage allowance, and seamless onward connections across its huge network if Istanbul is a stopover.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the budget carriers.

### British Airways & easyJet

**Typical fare 2026:** BA £120-£220 return (into IST); easyJet from Manchester.
**Pros:** BA offers Avios earning and a bag on standard fares into the convenient IST; easyJet adds a direct regional option from Manchester.
**Cons:** BA's cheapest "Basic" fares are cabin-only; easyJet's cheapest fares too.

**The honest read:** for the lowest price, Pegasus or AJet into SAW. For convenience, a bag included and the central IST, Turkish Airlines or BA. easyJet is handy for a direct Manchester departure. Compare the all-in price and factor which airport you land at.

| Airline | Cabin bag | Checked bag | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pegasus / AJet | Cabin bag on some fares | Paid add-on | Cheapest into SAW |

**The rule that saves the most:** add any hold bag at booking, never at the airport, and compare the all-in price. A £60 Pegasus fare into SAW plus a £45 hold bag is £105 — which can approach a Turkish Airlines fare into the more convenient IST that already includes a generous bag. Factor both the baggage and the airport into your comparison.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Nov):** Book **6-10 weeks out**.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug) and Easter:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — fixed demand, fares only rise.
- **Spring/autumn peaks (Apr-Jun, Sep-Oct):** Book **8-12 weeks out** for Istanbul's best sightseeing weather.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

The budget carriers run frequent sales, so set a price alert and book when Pegasus or AJet drops a fare.

## Common mistakes UK-Istanbul travellers make

1. **Still buying a Turkey e-visa.** UK travellers are now visa-free for tourism — don't pay for one you no longer need, and avoid scam "visa" sites.
2. **Booking a cheap SAW fare without checking the transfer.** Sabiha Gökçen is on the Asian side — the transfer to the European sights is long; factor it in.
3. **Judging fares on the headline price.** A Pegasus fare plus a bag can approach a Turkish Airlines fare into the more convenient IST that includes one.
4. **Booking summer late.** July-August is peak and hot — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
5. **Missing the spring/autumn value window.** Istanbul's best sightseeing weather at below peak prices.
6. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
7. **Checking a passport with too little validity.** Turkey wants ~150 days from entry — check before you book.
