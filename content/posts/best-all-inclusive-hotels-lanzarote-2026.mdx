Lanzarote is the quiet, design-led Canary — volcanic scenery, low-rise resorts and strong family value in Puerto del Carmen, with the shortest airport transfer of the islands. If you're weighing it against the other Canaries, [Tenerife](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-tenerife-2026) is bigger and livelier with more nightlife, while [Gran Canaria](/blog/best-all-inclusive-hotels-gran-canaria-2026) sits in between with big dunes and busy resorts. ## Lanzarote All-Inclusive FAQs

### Which is the cheapest area for an all-inclusive hotel in Lanzarote?

Most of the value sits in Puerto del Carmen, the island's biggest and most walkable resort, where the price gap is between hotel tiers rather than districts. On our midweek searches the cheapest bookable apartments start from around £38 a night, while the four-star all-inclusive hotels run from roughly £150 upward. Playa Blanca and Costa Teguise tend to price a touch higher for the same standard.

### How far is Puerto del Carmen from Lanzarote Airport (ACE)?

Puerto del Carmen sits about 10 km south-west of Lanzarote Airport (ACE), roughly a 10–15 minute drive — one of the shortest airport-to-resort transfers in the Canaries. That short hop is a big part of the island's appeal for families with young children and for long-weekend breaks.

### When is the best time to visit Lanzarote?

Lanzarote is a year-round sun destination, with daytime highs around 21–29°C even in winter. April to October brings the warmest sea and reliable pool weather, while November to March stays mild and is a favourite winter-sun escape for UK travellers. There's no true off-season, so prices flex with UK school holidays more than with the weather.

### Are there adults-only all-inclusive hotels in Lanzarote?

Yes — several Puerto del Carmen properties are adults-only, including IG Nachosol Atlantic & Yaizasol, Hotel Pocillos Playa, Apartamentos Panorama, Ereza Los Hibiscos and Apartamentos Isla de Lobos. They suit couples and friends who want a quieter, pool-and-sun stay away from kids' clubs.
