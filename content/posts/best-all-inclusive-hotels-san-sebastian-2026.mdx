## San Sebastián Hotel & All-Inclusive FAQs

### Are there true all-inclusive hotels in San Sebastián?

Board-basis all-inclusive is rare in San Sebastián — it's a city beach and foodie destination, not a mass resort belt like Benidorm or the Canaries. A handful of the bigger four-stars offer half-board or all-inclusive packages on certain dates, but most stays here are room-only or bed-and-breakfast, with pintxos bars a few steps from the door. Check each hotel's live page for its current board options on your dates.

### Which airport do I fly into for San Sebastián?

Fly into **Bilbao Airport (BIO)**, about 100 km west with the best UK connections and a direct coach into the city in around 75 minutes. The tiny **San Sebastián Airport (EAS)** at Hondarribia is closer but mainly runs domestic Spanish routes with very few UK flights. Biarritz (BIQ) across the French border is another option. [Search UK flights to Bilbao (BIO)](/flights?to=BIO).

### Which is the best area to stay?

The **Old Town (Parte Vieja)** is the pintxos heart; **Centro / Área Romántica** is the elegant core by La Concha; **Gros** sits by Zurriola surf beach with a younger, calmer vibe; and **Amara** is quieter and better value near the stations. All are walkable or a short bus from the bay.

### How much should I budget per night?

On our midweek searches, budget hotels and hostels start from around £118, mid-range four-stars run roughly £180–£450, and the landmark five-stars climb from £830 upward. San Sebastián is one of Spain's pricier cities, especially in summer and during the September film festival, so booking early pays off.
