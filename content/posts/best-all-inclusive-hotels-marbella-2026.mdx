## Marbella All-Inclusive FAQs

### Does Marbella have true all-inclusive hotels?

Marbella skews toward luxury spa resorts and four-star beach hotels rather than the big buffet-village all-inclusive model. Several properties here — AluaSun Marbella Park, NH Collection Marbella and a number of club resorts — offer an all-inclusive board option, but many flagship five-stars are room-only, B&B or half-board. Always check each hotel's live page for its exact board before booking.

### How far is Marbella from Málaga Airport (AGP)?

Marbella sits about 55 km west of Málaga Airport (AGP), roughly a 40-45 minute drive along the AP-7 coastal motorway. It's one of the easiest airport-to-resort hops on the Costa del Sol, with private transfers, shared shuttles, the direct bus and hire cars all serving the route. Puerto Banús and San Pedro are a few minutes further west.

### What is the Golden Mile in Marbella?

The Golden Mile is the prestigious coastal strip running from central Marbella west toward Puerto Banús, lined with gated villas, beach clubs and the town's most famous luxury resorts — Marbella Club, Puente Romano and the Alanda and Boho Club properties among them. It's where Marbella's upscale reputation was built, so expect the highest rates and the best beachfront.

### Is Marbella good for luxury and spa breaks?

Very — it's one of Marbella's strongest suits. The town is home to some of Spain's most celebrated resorts, including Marbella Club, Puente Romano and Boho Club, alongside wellness hotels like Alanda Marbella and ME by Meliá. Turkish-style spas, golf-and-spa combinations and Michelin-level dining cluster along the Golden Mile, making it a natural fit for couples wanting a polished, indulgent week.

### Which Marbella hotels are best value?

For value, look at the well-reviewed three- and four-star town hotels — Barceló Marbella, Sercotel VIME La Reserva de Marbella, Óbal Hotel Marbella and Hotel El Faro Marbella pair thousands of reviews with mid-band rates. At the cheapest end, Hotel Doña Catalina and the aparthotels near San Pedro and Elviria offer solid beds well under £200 a night on our searches.
