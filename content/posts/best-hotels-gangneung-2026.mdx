## UK Practicalities

- **Getting there**: Fly London to Seoul Incheon (ICN) — Korean Air and Asiana fly direct in about 11 hours; one-stop options via the Gulf or Asia are often cheaper. [Search flights to ICN](/flights?to=ICN). From Seoul, the KTX reaches Gangneung in under two hours.
- **Getting around Gangneung**: Local buses and taxis link the station, Gyeongpo Beach, Anmok Coffee Street and Jeongdongjin. A T-money card works on buses. A hire car helps for coastal pensions and the Pyeongchang mountains.
- **Visa**: UK travellers need a K-ETA (electronic travel authorisation) for visa-free entry — apply online before you fly.
- **Money**: Korean won (KRW). Convenience stores (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven) are everywhere for cheap eats. No accommodation tax; 10% VAT is already in the price.
- **Best months**: June and September for beach weather without peak crowds; July–August is warm but busy and pricier; autumn foliage and Seollal/Chuseok holidays spike rates. Winter is cheapest and quietest.
