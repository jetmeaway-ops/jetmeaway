## Hiroshima Hotels FAQs

### What's the cheapest area to stay in Hiroshima?

The Yokogawa and Funairi neighbourhoods just west of the centre have the cheapest rooms — OUCHI HOTEL Yokogawa and J-Hoppers Hiroshima Guesthouse both start around £41 a night. For the best value that's still walkable to the sights, look at the streets between Hiroshima Station and the Peace Park, where dozens of business hotels sit in the £50–90 band and every tram line runs through. You're never more than a 15-minute tram ride from anywhere you'd want to go.

### How cheap are hotels in Hiroshima?

Real, bookable rooms start at about £41 a night on midweek dates — that's OUCHI HOTEL Yokogawa and the J-Hoppers guesthouse. Capsule-style and hostel beds like Pinpon Hotel (~£45) and CUBIC.30 (~£51) sit just above. Standard business hotels near Hiroshima Station run £60–90, and even the two 5-star options top out around £185, so Hiroshima is one of the cheaper big Japanese cities for a hotel.

### Are there capsule or hostel options under £60 in Hiroshima?

Yes. J-Hoppers Hiroshima Guesthouse and OUCHI HOTEL Yokogawa start around £41, Pinpon Hotel Hiroshima from ~£45, Santiago Hotel from ~£52, and Hotel Pao (with more than 2,000 reviews) from ~£56. CUBIC.30 (~£51) offers compact pod-style rooms. All are central enough to reach the Peace Park and Hiroshima Station by tram in under 20 minutes.

### Is there a hotel or accommodation tax in Hiroshima?

Japan has no nationwide bed tax, and as of 2026 Hiroshima City does not levy its own accommodation tax — unlike Tokyo, Osaka or Kyoto, which add a small per-night charge on pricier rooms. Consumption tax (10%) is already included in the rates you see, so the price shown on our hotel pages is close to the all-in figure. Always check the total at checkout for any onsen or bathing surcharge.

### What are the best budget hotels in Hiroshima for families?

For families, the apartment-style OUCHI HOTEL branches (Yokogawa, Takeya, Otemachi, Hatchobori) give you more space than a business-hotel twin, and Natural Hot Spring Hotel Livemax Premium Hiroshima (~£61) has a real hot-spring bath the kids will remember. HOTEL MYSTAYS Hiroshima Peace Park (~£64, 6,000+ reviews) and Hotel S-Plus Hiroshima Peace Park (~£72) are steps from the park and offer larger rooms. All keep the whole trip under budget.

### What are the cheapest months to visit Hiroshima?

January, February and June (before the summer holidays) have the lowest room rates — often 20–30% below spring. Avoid the Golden Week holidays in early May and the Obon week in mid-August, when prices spike nationwide. Cherry-blossom season (late March to early April) and the autumn leaves (November) are the busiest and priciest, so book those months well ahead.

### How close are budget hotels to Hiroshima Station and the tram lines?

Most of the budget tier sits within a 15-minute walk or a single tram ride of Hiroshima Station. APA Hotel Hiroshima Ekimae Ohashi (12,000+ reviews) and Nest Hotel Hiroshima Ekimae are right by the station; Sotetsu Fresa Inn and Smile Hotel Hiroshima are a few tram stops away. The Hiroden tram network is flat-fare and covers everywhere you'd stay, so even a hotel a mile out is an easy hop to the platforms.

### Where should I stay in Hiroshima for the first time?

Stay between Hiroshima Station and the Hondori shopping arcade — you're walkable to the Peace Memorial Park and Museum, on the tram line to the Miyajima ferry, and surrounded by okonomiyaki restaurants. Business hotels like Mitsui Garden Hotel Hiroshima, Chisun Hotel Hiroshima and Comfort Hotel Hiroshima Otemachi in the £60–80 band put you in the middle of everything for a first visit.

### How do I get from Hiroshima to Miyajima and the floating torii?

Take the Hiroden tram or the JR Sanyo Line to Miyajimaguchi (about 30 minutes from central Hiroshima), then the ferry across to Miyajima island (10 minutes). The JR ferry is covered by the Japan Rail Pass. From most hotels in this guide the whole trip is under an hour, so the great torii gate of Itsukushima Shrine is an easy half-day. Aim for high tide to see it appear to float.

### Is Hiroshima a good base for the Shinkansen and day trips?

Excellent. Hiroshima Station is a major stop on the Sanyo Shinkansen — Osaka is about 1 hour 25 minutes, Kyoto around 1 hour 40, and Hakata (Fukuoka) roughly an hour away. Staying near the station lets you day-trip across western Japan and return the same night, which is why the Ekimae-area hotels are so popular with rail-pass travellers.

### How much does a mid-range hotel in Hiroshima cost?

The mid-range tier runs roughly £64–£124 a night on midweek dates. Mitsui Garden Hotel Hiroshima starts around £64, several 4-star names (Grand Prince, Rihga Royal, Oriental) sit in the £85 band, and the Hilton Hiroshima tops the tier near £124. All include breakfast options and are within a tram ride of the Peace Park.

### What's the best luxury hotel in Hiroshima?

The Sheraton Grand Hiroshima Hotel is the city's headline 5-star — directly connected to Hiroshima Station, with nearly 2,000 reviews, a spa and city views. It's the easiest luxury base for rail travellers. HOTEL CLA-SS HIROSHIMA-DOBASHI is a smaller design-led 5-star from around £84, offering boutique style at a much lower price point in the Dobashi area near Hondori.

### Are budget hotels in Hiroshima near the Peace Memorial Park?

Several are right beside it. HOTEL LiVEMAX Hiroshima Peace Park Mae (~£60), HOTEL MYSTAYS Hiroshima Peace Park (~£64) and Hotel S-Plus Hiroshima Peace Park (~£72) all sit within a short walk of the Peace Memorial Park and Museum, so you can visit the A-Bomb Dome first thing before the crowds. All three are under £75 a night.

### Do I need a car in Hiroshima?

No — the Hiroden tram network, JR lines and buses cover the city and the Miyajima ferry, and everything central is walkable. A car is only worth it for rural day trips inland. Stay near a tram line or Hiroshima Station and you'll never need one, which keeps costs down.

### What's the food to try in Hiroshima?

Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki — a layered savoury pancake with noodles, cabbage and egg, cooked on a hot plate — is the signature dish, best eaten at Okonomimura or a backstreet counter near Hondori. Oysters from the Seto Inland Sea are the other local speciality, at their best in winter. Both are cheap, which suits a budget trip.

### Are hotels in Hiroshima safe for solo travellers?

Yes — Hiroshima is a calm, mid-sized city with very low crime, and solo and female travellers move around comfortably day and night. The hostels and guesthouses (J-Hoppers, 36hostel, Pinpon) are set up for solo backpackers with common rooms and English-speaking staff. Standard city awareness is all that's needed.

### How far in advance should I book a Hiroshima hotel?

For cherry-blossom season (late March–April), the autumn leaves (November) and the Golden Week and Obon holidays, book two to three months ahead — the good-value business hotels sell out first. For the rest of the year, a few weeks is usually fine, and midweek availability is strong. Our hotel pages show live rates so you can see how far out the deals hold.

### Which hotels in Hiroshima have onsen or hot-spring baths?

Natural Hot Spring Hotel Livemax Premium Hiroshima (~£61) has a genuine natural hot-spring bath — rare at this price. Dormy Inn Hiroshima Annex (~£108) is known chain-wide for its top-floor public bath and free late-night noodles. Both are a treat after a day of walking, and cost far less than a countryside ryokan.

### Is Hiroshima expensive compared to Tokyo or Kyoto?

It's noticeably cheaper. Budget rooms start around £41 versus £50+ in Tokyo, mid-range business hotels are £60–90, and there's no city accommodation tax. Food — okonomiyaki, oysters, ramen — is inexpensive too. For budget travellers, Hiroshima is one of the best-value stops on a Japan rail trip.

### Can I walk to the A-Bomb Dome and Peace Park from these hotels?

From the Peace Park cluster (LiVEMAX Peace Park Mae, MYSTAYS Peace Park, S-Plus Peace Park) it's a few minutes on foot. From the Hondori and Otemachi hotels it's a 10–15 minute walk. From Hiroshima Station it's about 15 minutes by tram. The A-Bomb Dome, the Peace Memorial Museum and the Cenotaph are all in one riverside area you can cover on foot.

### Are there apartment-style hotels in Hiroshima for longer stays?

Yes — the OUCHI HOTEL branches (Yokogawa, Takeya, Otemachi, Hatchobori, Nagarekawa) are apartment-style with kitchenettes, ideal for a few nights or a week and better value for families or groups than separate rooms. CasaEn and bHotel offer similar self-contained stays. Rates run £41–£85 depending on branch and size.

### What's the best hotel right at Hiroshima Station?

The Sheraton Grand Hiroshima Hotel is directly connected to the station's north exit — the most convenient base for Shinkansen travellers. For budget, APA Hotel Hiroshima Ekimae Ohashi (12,000+ reviews, ~£73) and Nest Hotel Hiroshima Ekimae are steps away. All three let you roll your suitcase off the bullet train and into reception.

### Do hotels in Hiroshima have English-speaking staff?

The international chains (Sheraton, Hilton, ANA Crowne Plaza) and the larger business hotels have English-speaking front desks, and the backpacker hostels (J-Hoppers, 36hostel) are run for an international crowd. Smaller apartment-style places may rely on translation apps, but Hiroshima is well used to visitors and signage on the trams and at the sights is bilingual.

### Which budget hotel has the most reviews in Hiroshima?

APA Hotel Hiroshima Ekimae Ohashi leads with more than 12,000 reviews — a reliable, no-surprises business hotel by the station from around £73. Smile Hotel Hiroshima (7,300+), Sotetsu Fresa Inn (6,900+) and HOTEL MYSTAYS Hiroshima Peace Park (6,000+) are the other heavily-reviewed budget picks, all under £100.

### Are there hotels near Mazda Zoom-Zoom Stadium for baseball fans?

Yes — fav HIROSHIMA STADIUM and b hotel Ball Park are aimed at Hiroshima Carp baseball fans near the ballpark district. The stadium is a short walk from Hiroshima Station, so most Ekimae-area hotels work for a game too. Book early around home fixtures, when rooms fill fast.
