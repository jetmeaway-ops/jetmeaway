## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Nice?** **easyJet** — the lowest headline fares (from ~£29-£55 off-peak), with a growing France network for 2026. **BA** from Heathrow/Gatwick; **Jet2** from the regions with baggage included.

**How long is the flight?** About **2 hours** from London, similar from the Midlands, a little more from the north.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport on easyJet, but Nice flies direct from Manchester, Birmingham and Leeds too — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer and the May event weekends.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### easyJet — the price and network leader

**Typical fare 2026:** £29-£70 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**UK airports:** Gatwick, Luton, Stansted and, new for summer 2026, Birmingham; plus regional bases.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares and a growing France network for 2026. Frequent flights, regular sales, ideal for a hand-luggage Riviera weekend.
**Cons:** Hold bag, allocated seat and speedy boarding all cost extra.

### British Airways — full-service

**Typical fare 2026:** £75-£150 return; cheapest "Basic" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** From Heathrow and Gatwick, Avios earning, a bag on standard fares.
**Cons:** Pricier than easyJet; check the fare type for baggage.

### Jet2 — the baggage-included regional option

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£140 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance.
**UK airports:** Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds Bradford.
**Pros:** Baggage often included, friendly leisure service, strong regional coverage — frequently the best all-in value if you're checking a bag.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare; fewer London departures.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, easyJet wins on price. Checking a bag or flying from the north, Jet2's all-in fare is often better value. BA suits Avios collectors from Heathrow.

## Riviera events that spike Nice fares

Nice is the nearest major airport to both Cannes and Monaco, so the Riviera's big events lift fares and hotels across the whole coast:

- **Cannes Film Festival** (May) — huge international demand; the entire coast fills.
- **Monaco Grand Prix** (May) — one of the priciest weekends of the year for Nice flights and hotels.
- **Nice Carnival** (February) — a major local draw that lifts mid-February demand.

If you're attending any of these, book well ahead. If you're not, check the dates and avoid those specific weeks — fares fall straight back to normal outside them.
