## Lloret de Mar All-Inclusive FAQs

### Which airport do I fly into for Lloret de Mar?

Girona-Costa Brava Airport (GRO) is the closest gateway, roughly a 30-40 minute transfer from Lloret. Barcelona-El Prat (BCN) is the busier alternative about 75-90 minutes away, with far more UK routes and year-round flights. Search UK flights to GRO or BCN and compare live Lloret hotel prices to line up your dates.

### Is Lloret de Mar good for families or better for nightlife?

Both — and you can pick your base to suit. Central hotels near the main beach put you in the middle of the bars and clubs, ideal for groups and older travellers. The Fenals and Santa Cristina sides are noticeably quieter and calmer, so families can stay close to the sand while keeping the nightlife at arm's length. Many four-stars run a full all-inclusive board that suits both.

### Which is the best beach in Lloret de Mar?

Lloret Beach is the long central sandy stretch backed by the promenade, bars and hotels — the busiest and most convenient. Fenals Beach, just west, is broader and calmer, good for families. Smaller coves like Santa Cristina and Cala Boadella are more scenic and sheltered. Many hotels sit a short walk or shuttle from the sand — check each hotel's live page for its exact position.

### Are there budget all-inclusive hotels in Lloret de Mar?

Yes — Lloret has one of the deepest budget tiers of any Spanish resort. Beyond the four-stars, there are hostals, smart hotels and apartments from around £91 a night, with most budget rooms between £110 and £180 (up to roughly £195 at the top of the tier) on our midweek searches. Some run an all-inclusive board while others are bed-and-breakfast or room-only, so check each hotel's board on its live page. It's ideal for groups and cost-focused families.
