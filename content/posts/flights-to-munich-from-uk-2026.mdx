## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Munich?** **easyJet** — the lowest headline fares (from ~£30-£55 off-peak). **Lufthansa** has the most flights with a bag included; **BA** is the most punctual.

**How long is the flight?** About **1 hour 55 minutes** from the UK.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport, but Munich flies direct from Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Glasgow on Lufthansa — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for Oktoberfest, the Christmas markets and trade-fair weeks.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

<a id="airline-comparison"></a>

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### easyJet — the price leader

**Typical fare 2026:** £30-£70 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**UK airports:** Gatwick, Luton, Stansted and regional bases.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares, frequent flights, regular sales. Ideal for a hand-luggage Bavaria weekend.
**Cons:** Hold bag, allocated seat and speedy boarding all cost extra.

### Lufthansa — the full-service leader

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£140 return; standard fares include a bag.
**UK airports:** Heathrow (the most flights, around 61 a week), plus Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Glasgow.
**Pros:** The widest UK network to Munich (its hub), a bag on standard fares, seamless onward connections worldwide. Cheapest "Economy Light" fares are cabin-only, so check.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than easyJet.

### British Airways

**Typical fare 2026:** £75-£140 return; "Basic" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** The most punctual carrier on the route, Avios earning, a bag on standard fares, from Heathrow.
**Cons:** Pricier than easyJet.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, easyJet wins on price. Checking a bag, flying from a regional airport, or wanting onward connections, Lufthansa's standard fare is often better value than it looks. BA suits punctuality and Avios collectors.

<a id="events"></a>

## Oktoberfest, Christmas markets and trade fairs

Three event periods lift Munich fares well above baseline:

- **Oktoberfest** (mid-to-late September into early October) — the world's biggest beer festival and Munich's single largest travel spike. Flights and hotels surge; book 3-4 months ahead if you're going.
- **December Christmas markets** — Marienplatz and the citywide markets make December atmospheric and popular.
- **Trade fairs** at Messe München — major international fairs fill the city with business travellers on specific weeks.

If your dates are flexible and you're not attending, check the calendar and avoid these; if you are, book well ahead.

## Common mistakes UK-Munich travellers make

1. **Booking Oktoberfest late.** It's Munich's biggest spike — book 3-4 months ahead, or avoid the dates to save.
2. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Munich flies direct from Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Glasgow on Lufthansa — your local one may be cheapest.
3. **Judging easyJet on the headline fare.** Add a hold bag and a Lufthansa fare that includes one (plus connections) can win.
4. **Ignoring trade-fair weeks.** Major fairs spike hotels and fares — check the calendar.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Not planning a Bavaria day trip.** Neuschwanstein and the Alps are easy from Munich by train — build one in.
