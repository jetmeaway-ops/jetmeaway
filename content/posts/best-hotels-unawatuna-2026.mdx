Two to four nights is a comfortable stay. That gives you time for beach and snorkelling days in the bay, a walk to Jungle Beach and the Peace Pagoda, and at least one easy day trip into Galle Fort. Many travellers use Unawatuna as a relaxed anchor for a wider south-coast trip taking in Mirissa and Weligama too.

**Is Unawatuna safe for tourists?**
Unawatuna is a popular, well-established tourist beach village and generally feels relaxed and welcoming. Normal travel awareness applies — watch belongings on the beach, take care with sea currents outside the calm season, and use reputable transfers. Check the latest UK Foreign Office advice for Sri Lanka before you travel.

**Can I combine Unawatuna with other south-coast beaches?**
Easily. Unawatuna sits at the western end of Sri Lanka's south-coast beach belt, with Mirissa and Weligama a short drive further east — both known for surf, whale watching and their own laid-back beaches. Galle Fort is minutes away in the other direction. It's simple to string these together into one relaxed south-coast trip.
