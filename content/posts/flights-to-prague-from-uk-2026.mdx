## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair, easyJet & Wizz Air — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £17-£50 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. Ryanair runs the most flights (around 35 a week); easyJet and Wizz add strong frequency from London and the regions. Ideal for a hand-luggage weekend.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra.

### British Airways — full-service

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£130 return; "Basic" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** From Heathrow (around 33 flights a week), Avios earning, a bag on standard fares.
**Cons:** Pricier than the budget carriers.

### Jet2 — the regional, baggage-included option

**Typical fare 2026:** £60-£120 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance.
**Pros:** Baggage often included, friendly service, strong regional UK coverage — frequently the best all-in value from outside London or if you're checking a bag.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the ultra-budget carriers.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair, easyJet or Wizz win on price. From a regional airport or checking a bag, Jet2's all-in fare is often better value. BA suits Avios collectors from Heathrow.

## Money in Prague: koruna, not euro

A quick but useful point: **the Czech Republic keeps its own currency, the Czech koruna (CZK)** — it is not in the euro despite being in the EU and Schengen. In 2026 the rate has averaged around **28 koruna to the pound**.

- **Prague is excellent value** for UK visitors — beer, food and going out cost far less than at home.
- **Pay in koruna**, not euros — venues that accept euros usually give a poor rate.
- When a card machine offers to **charge you in pounds instead of koruna, always choose koruna** (declining dynamic currency conversion avoids a hidden markup).
- **Avoid the exchange kiosks** in the tourist centre, which often have bad rates and fees — use a bank ATM or pay by card.

## Common mistakes UK-Prague travellers make

1. **Assuming the Czech Republic uses the euro.** It's the koruna — pay by card in koruna and decline "pay in pounds."
2. **Changing money at tourist-centre kiosks.** Rates there are poor — use a bank ATM or a card.
3. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Prague flies from many UK airports — your local one is often cheapest.
4. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £25 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can be beaten by a Jet2 fare that includes baggage.
5. **Booking the Christmas markets late.** December is Prague's peak — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
6. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
7. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
