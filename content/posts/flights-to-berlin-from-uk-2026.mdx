## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Berlin?** **Ryanair, easyJet and Eurowings** — the lowest headline fares (from ~£17-£40 off-peak). **BA and Wizz** also fly.

**How long is the flight?** About **1 hour 50 minutes to 2 hours** from the UK.

**Which airport?** Just one — **Berlin Brandenburg (BER)** — which keeps things simple.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport on a budget carrier, but Berlin flies from many UK airports — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer, the Christmas markets and New Year.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

<a id="airline-comparison"></a>

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair, easyJet & Eurowings — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £17-£50 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. Ryanair from Stansted and Luton; easyJet from Gatwick, Luton and its regional bases; Eurowings adds capacity. Ideal for a hand-luggage weekend.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra.

### British Airways & Wizz Air

**Typical fare 2026:** BA £70-£140 return; Wizz budget model.
**Pros:** BA from Heathrow with Avios earning and a bag on standard fares; Wizz adds low-cost capacity on some routes.
**Cons:** BA's cheapest "Basic" fares are cabin-only; Wizz's cheapest fares too.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair, easyJet or Eurowings win on price. Need a checked bag, compare their all-in fares against BA's standard fare, which includes one. For a weekend, travel cabin-only and the budget fare stays tiny.

For regional travellers, always price your local direct flight before assuming a cheap London fare wins once the London trek is added.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Nov):** Book **4-8 weeks out**.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug), Christmas markets, New Year:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — fixed demand, fares only rise.
- **Event/trade-fair dates:** Book early — Berlin's big fairs and concerts spike hotels too.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

## Common mistakes UK-Berlin travellers make

1. **Looking for Tegel or Schönefeld.** Both closed — all Berlin flights now use BER.
2. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Berlin flies from many UK airports — your local one is often cheapest.
3. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £30 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can match a BA fare that includes one.
4. **Booking the Christmas markets late.** December is Berlin's peak — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Not buying the right transport ticket.** BER is in Berlin's ABC fare zone — buy the correct ticket for the airport train.
