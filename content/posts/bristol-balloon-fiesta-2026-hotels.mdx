## The Bristol Balloon Fiesta 2026 — the essentials

- **When:** Friday 7 – Sunday 9 August 2026 (the 48th edition). Check the official site for the day-by-day schedule, as a preview evening is sometimes added.
- **Where:** Ashton Court Estate, on the south-west edge of Bristol, across the Avon Gorge from Clifton.
- **Cost:** Free to attend — one of Europe's largest free events, with around 250,000 visitors.
- **The highlights:** two **mass ascents** a day, at dawn (around 6am) and dusk (around 6pm), with 100+ hot-air balloons; and the world-famous **Night Glows**, where tethered balloons light up to music, followed by fireworks.
- **The catch for your trip:** the ascents only fly in the right weather, the site and roads are busiest around the Night Glows, and **accommodation sells out months ahead**. The dawn ascent is the hardest to reach from far out — which is why a Clifton or central base pays off.

## Parking & getting there (the bit everyone gets wrong)

**Car parking is advance-only — there are no tickets on the gate.** Buy on the [official fiesta site](https://www.bristolballoonfiesta.co.uk/car-park-tickets) before you travel.

- **Prices:** around **£8 adult / £5 child / £22 group of up to five**.
- **Blue Gate (Gate 4)** — the closest car park, adjacent to the site with a short walk, opening at **5am** for the morning launch. Best if you're doing the dawn ascent.
- **Red Gate** — the main public car park, about a **1.5km downhill walk** to the arena.
- **Long Ashton Park & Ride** — linked to Bristol Temple Meads by the **m2 metrobus**; the smart choice for drivers who'd rather not queue for the site car parks. (Coach parking runs Saturday only.)

**Car-free is often easier:**
- **Train to Bristol Temple Meads** (GWR from London Paddington ≈ 1h20), then the **festival shuttle bus** from the station and city centre (from 10am), or **cycle the traffic-free "Festival Way"**.
- **Driving in:** from the **north, M5 Junction 19** (A369); from the **south, M5 Junction 21** (A370). **Do not drive through the city centre** — follow the event signs, and expect **road closures** around Ashton Court.

**The upshot for choosing a hotel:** if you want the dawn ascent without a car battle, stay in **Clifton** (closest) or **central** (shuttle from Temple Meads). If you're driving, pick an **edge-of-city hotel with parking near the M5** and use the park-and-ride for the site itself.

## Book early — Fiesta weekend sells out

With around 250,000 visitors over three days, Bristol's rooms — and especially the cheaper beds — sell out well ahead of **7–9 August 2026**, and remaining rooms climb in price. The order of scarcity is predictable:

- **Budget beds go first:** Almondsbury Inn (~£78), The Full Moon Backpackers (~£79), Best Western Henbury Lodge (~£98).
- **Clifton fills next** — it's the closest neighbourhood, so demand is highest.
- **Central and driver-edge hotels** hold on a little longer but still tighten as the weekend nears.

Two honest tips: the from-prices in this guide are **midweek rates** — Fiesta weekend runs higher, so [compare live prices](/hotels?city=Bristol) for your exact dates. And if Bristol is full, **Bath (≈15 min by train) or Cardiff (≈45–50 min)** work as fallbacks for the daytime and dusk ascents, though the 6am dawn ascent and late Night Glow are much easier from a Bristol base.
