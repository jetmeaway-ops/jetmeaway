## What to do on Gili Trawangan & Lombok

**Gili Trawangan** is the car-free party-and-snorkelling island off Lombok — snorkel with green and hawksbill turtles straight off the beach, dive the surrounding reefs, cycle the sandy loop, and catch the famous sunset swings out over the water. There are no cars or motorbikes: you walk, hire a bike, or take a cidomo pony cart. Nights bring the beach bars and the Gilis' well-known social scene. **Senggigi and the wider Lombok mainland** are the bigger, quieter neighbour — west-coast beaches and sunsets around Senggigi, the multi-day Mount Rinjani volcano trek, the Sendang Gile and Tiu Kelep waterfalls, and the pink-sand Pink Beach (Tangsi) down in the south. Together they make a natural island-plus-mainland pairing.
