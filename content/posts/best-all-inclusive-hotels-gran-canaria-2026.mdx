## Gran Canaria All-Inclusive FAQs

### Which airport do I fly into for Gran Canaria?

Gran Canaria Airport (LPA) is the island's single gateway, sitting on the east coast between Las Palmas in the north and the southern resorts. It handles direct flights from many UK airports year-round. From LPA it's roughly 20–25 minutes north to Las Palmas or 25–35 minutes south to Maspalomas and Playa del Inglés. Search UK flights to LPA and line them up with live hotel prices.

### Is Gran Canaria warm in winter?

Yes — this is the island's headline draw. Gran Canaria sits off the coast of northwest Africa and enjoys mild, sunny weather all year, with winter daytime highs typically around 20–23°C and plenty of sunshine. It's one of Europe's most reliable winter-sun destinations, which is why UK travellers book it from October through March as well as summer.

### What is Las Canteras beach like?

Las Canteras is the long golden-sand city beach that runs along the western edge of Las Palmas — a wide, walkable promenade lined with cafés and hotels, protected by a natural offshore reef that calms the water for swimming. It's consistently rated one of the best urban beaches in Europe, and many of the mid-range and budget hotels in this guide sit within a short walk of it.

### Do these Gran Canaria hotels include meals?

It varies by property. Some offer all-inclusive or half-board plans, but because Las Palmas is a city packed with restaurants, many hotels here are bed-and-breakfast or room-only by design. The board type is shown on each hotel's live JetMeAway page, so check there before booking if all-inclusive is a must-have. Self-catering apartments are also common and let you cook your own meals.
