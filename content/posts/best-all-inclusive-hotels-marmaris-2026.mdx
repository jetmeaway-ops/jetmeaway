Marmaris is the UK's classic Turkish all-inclusive escape — a big, buzzy resort town wrapped around a marina on a sheltered Aegean bay, backed by pine-covered hills, with a beach that runs for miles and a Bar Street that runs most of the night. It's where a huge share of British package holidaymakers have taken their first all-inclusive week in Turkey, and it's still one of the best-value sun-sea-and-buffet holidays in Europe. We've rebuilt this guide around all three price bands: **10 luxury resorts, 6 mid-range beach hotels, and 33 budget picks** we verified as real, currently bookable Marmaris properties — **49 hotels in all**, each linking straight to its live prices.

The town splits into two moods. **Marmaris centre** is the lively one — marina, town beach, shopping and the famous Bar Street on your doorstep. **Icmeler**, 15 minutes west around the bay, is the calmer, greener, more family-focused side, with a gentler beach and quieter evenings under the pines. Both share the same all-inclusive belt and the same Dalaman (DLM) airport transfer — roughly 90 minutes over the hills — so the choice is about the holiday you want, not the price you pay.

## Marmaris Essentials — Beyond the Beach

A few things worth planning around your all-inclusive week:

- **Marmaris marina and castle quarter** — the prettiest evening walk in town, around the yachts and up to the old Ottoman castle for the view over the bay.
- **Bar Street (Barlar Sokağı)** — the pedestrian nightlife strip in the centre, a short walk from the marina. The reason Marmaris is a nightlife name.
- **Boat trips around the bays** — daily departures from the harbour to the surrounding coves, plus the classic day trip to Dalyan's mud baths, rock tombs and İztuzu turtle beach.
- **Icmeler and the pine coves** — the quiet, family side of the bay, 15 minutes west, backed by pine hills and calmer water.
- **Marmaris cable car** — up into the hills behind town for a panoramic view over the whole bay.
- **Day trip to Rhodes** — the Greek island is a ferry ride from Marmaris harbour, a full day out to a different country and cuisine.

## UK Practicalities

- **Airport**: Dalaman (DLM), about 90 minutes' drive from Marmaris. Direct seasonal UK flights on TUI, Jet2, easyJet and others through summer. [Search flights to DLM](/flights?to=DLM). Pre-book a transfer or use the Havaş airport bus to Marmaris otogar.
- **Visa**: none required for UK tourists under 90 days — just a valid passport (check FCDO guidance before travelling).
- **Currency**: Turkish lira (TRY), though hotel rates are usually quoted in EUR/GBP. Bring lira or small euro notes for tips, taxis, dolmuş minibuses and off-resort spending.
- **Marmaris centre vs Icmeler**: centre for marina, shopping and Bar Street nightlife; Icmeler for a calmer, greener, family beach 15 minutes west. A cheap dolmuş links them all day.
- **Best months**: May, June, September and October (24–30°C, warm sea, thinner crowds). July–August is hottest and busiest. Shoulder months are the value sweet spot.
- **Alcohol and tipping**: alcohol is freely available and included on all-inclusive boards. Tipping isn't compulsory but a few lira for bar, restaurant and housekeeping staff is appreciated.
