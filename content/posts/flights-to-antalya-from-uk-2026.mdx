## Good news: no visa needed for 2026

For years, UK travellers to Turkey had to buy an e-visa before flying. That has changed: **UK passport holders no longer need a visa or e-visa to visit Turkey for tourism**, and can now enter **visa-free for stays of up to 90 days** in any 180-day period.

A few practical points:

- Your **passport should be valid for at least 150 days** from your date of entry and have a blank page for the stamp.
- The visa-free rule is for **tourism and short business trips** — different rules apply for work or long stays.
- Entry requirements can change, so **always confirm the current position** on the [UK government's Turkey travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/turkey) shortly before you travel.

This removes a small cost and admin step that used to catch travellers out at the last minute — one less thing to sort before an Antalya holiday.

## Common mistakes UK-Antalya travellers make

1. **Still buying a Turkey e-visa.** UK travellers are now visa-free for tourism — don't pay for a visa you no longer need (and avoid scam "visa" sites).
2. **Booking peak summer or school holidays late.** July-August is the priciest window — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
3. **Not comparing flight-only against a package.** Both can win depending on dates and hotel — check both.
4. **Underestimating the flight length.** At 4-5 hours it's longer than a typical Med beach flight — choose baggage and comfort accordingly.
5. **Booking a transfer without checking resort distance.** Alanya is ~2 hours from the airport; Belek and Side are much closer.
6. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A SunExpress/Pegasus fare plus a bag can match a Jet2 fare that includes one.
7. **Checking a passport with too little validity.** Turkey wants ~150 days' validity from entry — check before you book.
