## The Scout's Take: Patong, Old Town, or the Quieter Beaches?

**Patong** is Phuket's engine room — the main beach, the biggest choice of hotels at every price, and Bangla Road's neon nightlife. It's where most first-timers base themselves, and where the widest range of cheap-to-luxury rooms sits. If you want the beach, the boat tours and the buzz within walking distance, Patong wins. Pick a hotel a few streets back or on the quieter Kalim end if you want to sleep.

**Phuket Old Town (Phuket City)** is the cultural and budget heart — a walkable grid of Sino-Portuguese shophouses, street art, cafes and the island's best cheap food. This is where the hostels and guesthouses from £12 cluster, and where the Sunday Walking Street market runs. The trade-off is a 30–40 minute ride to the west-coast beaches.

**The quieter beaches** — Kata and Karon just south of Patong, plus the calmer Kalim and Tri Trang ends — trade nightlife for softer sand and family-friendly swimming. For first Phuket, base in Patong. For culture and the lowest prices, Old Town. For a calm beach holiday, Kata/Karon or Patong's quiet fringes — and this guide's [budget tier](#budget) is where £12–30 a night is still real.

## Phuket vs Krabi vs Koh Samui — Which Island?

Phuket is Thailand's biggest and most developed island, with the widest choice of hotels at every price, the best transport links (its own international airport) and the most nightlife and day-trip options — the natural first-timer choice. **[Krabi](/blog/best-hotels-krabi-2026)**, just across the bay, trades some of that infrastructure for more dramatic limestone-cliff scenery at Railay and Ao Nang, and slightly lower prices. **[Koh Samui](/blog/best-hotels-koh-samui-2026)**, in the Gulf on the other side of the country, has calmer beaches and a bigger wellness-retreat scene but a wetter October–December. For a first Thai island holiday with the most flexibility, Phuket wins; many UK travellers pair it with Krabi for a two-island trip.

## UK Practicalities

- **Flights**: No direct UK–Phuket flights. Connect via Bangkok (BKK/DMK) or a Gulf hub (Doha, Dubai, Abu Dhabi) then a 1.5-hour hop to Phuket (HKT). Total 14–18 hours. [Search flights to HKT](/flights?to=HKT).
- **Airport**: Phuket International (HKT) is at the north of the island — 45–60 minutes to Patong, ~40 to Old Town. Shared minibus (cheapest), Grab/taxi (~£18–28) or hotel transfer.
- **Visa**: UK passport holders currently get visa-exempt entry to Thailand for tourism (check the latest allowance before you fly). Passport valid at least 6 months.
- **Currency**: Thai baht (THB), cash-friendly — 7-Eleven and ATMs everywhere. Tipping is not obligatory but appreciated.
- **Getting around**: Grab app (transparent pricing), songthaew shared buses (cheapest), tuk-tuks/taxis (agree the fare first). Scooters are cheap but the roads are dangerous.
- **Best months**: November–February (cool, dry, peak) is the best weather; June–October (green season) is cheapest with short heavy showers; mid-April is Songkran.
- **Budget**: Backpacker — £30–45/day. Mid-range — £70–120/day. Luxury Patong resort — £150+/night before food and tours. Island-hopping day trips £25–60pp.
