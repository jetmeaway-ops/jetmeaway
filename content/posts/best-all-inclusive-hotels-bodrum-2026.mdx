## The Scout's Take: Which Bodrum Bay?

The **Bodrum peninsula** is a cluster of bays, each with its own character, all within a 45-minute transfer of the airport. Where you stay shapes the holiday more than which star rating you pick.

**Bodrum town** has the castle, the marina, the ancient theatre and the widest choice of hotels and nightlife — the best base for a first trip and for anyone who wants to walk to bars and restaurants. **Gümbet**, a mile west, is the lively budget strip with the cheapest all-inclusive beds and a busy beach. **Bitez**, 6km west, is calmer and family-friendly with a long beach and a windsurfing scene.

Further round the peninsula, **Torba** (north) is quiet and upmarket, while **Turgutreis** and **Yalıkavak** on the far side are the yacht-set marinas — smart, calm, and 20-30 minutes from town. For a first Bodrum trip, base in town or Bitez; for nightlife on a budget, Gümbet; for a quieter, more design-led stay, the outer bays.

## Beyond the Beach — Bodrum's Essentials

A few non-resort experiences worth planning around your stay:

- **Bodrum Castle & the Museum of Underwater Archaeology** — the 15th-century Castle of St Peter guards the harbour and houses one of the world's finest collections of ancient shipwreck finds.
- **The marina & yacht harbour** — Bodrum's superyacht scene is a spectacle in itself; walk the marina at sunset before dinner on the waterfront.
- **A gület boat trip** — the classic Bodrum day out, cruising the peninsula's bays on a traditional wooden gület with swim stops in turquoise coves.
- **Ferry to Kos** — the Greek Dodecanese island is a short hop across the water from Bodrum marina, an easy passport-in-pocket day trip.
- **Yalıkavak & Turgutreis marinas** — the smart, calm end of the peninsula, with upscale dining and shopping around the harbours.
- **Bodrum nightlife** — from beach clubs to the marina bars, Bodrum has Turkey's most famous after-dark scene.
- **The ancient theatre & windmills** — Hellenistic ruins above the town and the row of old windmills on the ridge, both with sweeping bay views.

## UK Practicalities

- **Direct UK flights**: Jet2, TUI, easyJet and BA fly direct to Bodrum-Milas (BJV) in season (late April–October); otherwise connect via Istanbul. [Search flights to BJV](/flights?to=BJV).
- **Airport**: Bodrum-Milas (BJV) is 35–45 minutes from Bodrum town and the main bays; Yalıkavak and Turgutreis are 45–55 minutes. Most resorts and packages include or offer a transfer.
- **Visa**: none required — UK passport holders can visit Turkey visa-free for tourist stays under 90 days. Check FCDO passport-validity guidance before you travel.
- **Currency**: Turkish lira (TRY), though hotel rates are usually quoted in EUR/USD/GBP. Bring lira or a card for tips, taxis, the market and anything off-resort.
- **Best months**: May, June, September and early October (24–30°C) for the sweet spot; July–August is hot and busiest; the season runs roughly late April to late October.
- **Budget**: budget-tier trip — £52–160/night; mid-range 4★ — £87–203/night; luxury 5★ — £196–680/night. All-inclusive settles most food and drink; ultra-board resorts settle almost everything.
