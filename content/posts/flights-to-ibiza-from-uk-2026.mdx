## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Ibiza?** **Ryanair and easyJet** on headline price (from ~£16-£45 shoulder-season), with **Jet2** running the most flights and best baggage-included value. **BA and TUI** also fly.

**How long is the flight?** About **2 hours 30 minutes** from London, a little more from northern airports.

**Is it seasonal?** Very — lots of flights **May-October**, few in winter. Book early for any off-season trip.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Whichever is **nearest** — Ibiza flies from many UK airports in summer.

**When should I book?** Around **6 weeks** ahead for the best shoulder fares; **8-12 weeks** for peak July-August and party weekends.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

<a id="seasonality"></a>

## Why Ibiza is so seasonal

More than almost any other Spanish route, Ibiza is a **summer-season destination**, and its flight schedule reflects that.

- **Peak summer (June-September):** many daily flights from across the UK on all five carriers — this is when the island is fully open, and when fares are highest.
- **Shoulder season (May, early June, late September, October):** good weather, most venues still open, and the **best balance of availability and price** — often the smartest time to go.
- **Winter (November-April):** the schedule shrinks dramatically to a handful of routes, much of the nightlife and many hotels close, and choice is limited. A winter Ibiza trip is a quiet, off-season experience — book early because options are few.

If your dates are flexible, the shoulder months are the sweet spot; if you're going for the clubbing scene, note that the **opening and closing party weekends** (roughly May and October) are the most in-demand and priciest dates of the whole year.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & easyJet — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £16-£60 return shoulder-season, hand-luggage only; much more at peak.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. easyJet flies from Gatwick, Luton and Southend; Ryanair from Stansted. Ideal for a hand-luggage party weekend.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra.

### Jet2 — the most flights, baggage included

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£150 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance.
**Pros:** Runs the most flights on the route (around 78 a week), baggage often included, friendly leisure service, strong regional coverage — frequently the best all-in value for a longer summer stay with checked bags.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the ultra-budget carriers.

### British Airways & TUI

**Typical fare 2026:** BA £70-£160 return; TUI often sold as part of a package.
**Pros:** BA (around 49 flights a week) adds frequency and Avios earning; TUI is convenient if you want flights bundled with a hotel.
**Cons:** BA's cheapest fares are cabin-only; TUI flight-only availability can be limited.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only for a weekend, Ryanair or easyJet win. For a longer summer stay with bags, Jet2's all-in fare and frequency are hard to beat. BA suits Avios collectors; TUI suits package travellers.

## UK airport comparison — fly local

In summer, Ibiza is served from a wide spread of UK airports, so the cheapest and easiest option is usually your **nearest** one. London (Gatwick, Luton, Stansted, Southend) has the most choice, but Jet2 and TUI add strong regional coverage from Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and beyond. Always price your local direct flight first — on this route it rarely pays to travel to a bigger airport. Outside summer, options narrow sharply and you may have fewer local choices.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Shoulder season (May, Jun, late Sep, Oct):** Book **around 6 weeks out** for the best balance of price and open venues.
- **Peak summer (Jul-Aug):** Book **8-12 weeks out** — the most in-demand dates of the year.
- **Opening & closing party weekends:** Book as early as possible — these spike hardest.
- **Midweek departures (Tue/Wed/Sat):** Often cheaper than the Friday-evening rush.

Set a price alert for your dates and book when a budget carrier drops a sale fare.

<a id="common-mistakes"></a>

## Common mistakes UK-Ibiza travellers make

1. **Assuming winter flights are plentiful.** Ibiza's schedule shrinks dramatically off-season — book early and expect few options.
2. **Booking peak July-August or party weekends late.** These are the priciest dates of the year — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
3. **Missing the shoulder-season value window.** May, June, late September and October offer open venues and lower fares.
4. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £40 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can be beaten by a Jet2 fare that includes baggage.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Not pre-booking a transfer for a big group.** Peak-night taxi queues can be long.
