## Beyond the Beach — Busan's Essentials

A few experiences worth planning around your stay:

- **Gamcheon Culture Village** — the hillside maze of pastel houses, murals and tiny galleries above western Busan. Go in the morning before the crowds and the heat.
- **Jagalchi Fish Market & Nampo** — Korea's largest seafood market: pick your fish downstairs, eat it upstairs. BIFF Square and Gukje Market are steps away.
- **Haeundae & Gwangalli beaches** — the two headline stretches of sand; Gwangalli faces the Gwangan (Diamond) Bridge, lit at night.
- **Taejongdae** — the forested cliff park on Yeongdo island with sea views and a lighthouse; take the Danubi road-train around the loop.
- **Songdo Cable Car & Cloud Walk** — a glass-floored coastal walk and a cable car over the sea at Songdo beach.
- **Beomeosa Temple** — a serene mountain temple on the slopes of Geumjeongsan, a world away from the beaches.

## UK Practicalities

- **Flights**: There are no direct UK–Busan flights; most UK travellers connect via Seoul Incheon (then a 2.5-hour KTX or a short domestic flight) or a hub like Doha, Dubai or Istanbul into Busan Gimhae (PUS). [Search flights to Busan](/flights?to=PUS).
- **Airport**: Gimhae International (PUS) is west of the city — light rail plus subway, or the airport limousine bus to Haeundae, Seomyeon and Busan Station for a few pounds.
- **Getting around**: A four-line subway plus buses and cheap taxis; a T-money card works on all of them. KTX high-speed rail links Busan to Seoul (~2.5h) and Gyeongju is an easy day trip.
- **Best months**: Late spring and autumn for mild weather; summer (July–August) is beach-peak and humid; November–March is cheapest and clear but cold. Avoid the Chuseok and Seollal holiday spikes.
- **Budget**: Beach-tower trip — £140–370/night. Budget-tier trip — £18–50/night, with market and convenience-store meals for a few pounds. A 4-night budget Busan trip built on this guide's cheap tier can land well under £250 per person before flights.
