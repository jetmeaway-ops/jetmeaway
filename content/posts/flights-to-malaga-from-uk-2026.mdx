## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Malaga?** **Ryanair** — most flights and lowest headline fares (from ~£16-£40 off-peak) — with **easyJet** close behind. **Jet2** for baggage-included beach-holiday value; **BA** full-service.

**How long is the flight?** About **2 hours 50 minutes** from London and most of the UK; **Bristol** is one of the fastest at ~2h30-2h40.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Whichever is **nearest** — Malaga flies from dozens of UK airports, so flying local almost always wins.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer, Easter and the shoulder peaks.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & easyJet — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £16-£50 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The two highest-frequency carriers (around 160 and 139 flights a week), with the lowest headline fares and the widest UK airport spread. Ideal for a hand-luggage city break.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra — the fare climbs once you check a bag for a beach week.

### Jet2 — the beach-holiday all-rounder

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£140 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance.
**UK airports:** Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds Bradford, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Newcastle, East Midlands, Bristol.
**Pros:** Baggage often included, friendly leisure service, strong regional coverage — frequently the best all-in value for a family beach holiday with checked bags.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the ultra-budget carriers; fewer London departures.

### British Airways — full-service

**Typical fare 2026:** £80-£160 return; cheapest "Basic" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** From Heathrow (occasionally Gatwick), Avios earning, a bag on standard fares.
**Cons:** Pricier than the budget carriers; fewer frequencies than the leisure specialists.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair or easyJet win on price. For a family beach holiday with checked bags, Jet2's all-in fare is often the best value and its regional coverage is excellent. BA suits Avios collectors flying from Heathrow.

<a id="uk-airports"></a>

## UK airport comparison — fly local

Malaga is served from an exceptionally wide spread of UK airports, so the cheapest and easiest option is almost always your **nearest** one. easyJet, Ryanair and Jet2 between them cover Gatwick, Luton, Stansted, Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Leeds Bradford, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Liverpool, Newcastle and East Midlands, among others.

**Bristol travellers** have a small bonus: its south-westerly position gives one of the shortest flight times to Malaga of any major UK airport. Wherever you are, price your local direct flight first — on this route it rarely pays to travel to a bigger airport.

## Baggage compared, honestly

**The rule that saves the most:** add any hold bag at booking, never at the airport, and compare the all-in price. A £30 Ryanair fare plus a £45 return hold bag is £75 — which for a beach holiday with checked bags can be beaten by a Jet2 fare that already includes baggage. For a city-only weekend, travel hand-luggage only and the budget fare stays tiny.

<a id="booking-windows"></a>

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Nov):** Book **4-8 weeks out**.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug), Easter, school holidays:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — peak Costa del Sol demand, fares only rise.
- **Shoulder peaks (May-Jun, Sep):** Book **6-10 weeks out** for the best balance of beach weather and price.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

Set a price alert for your dates and book when a budget carrier drops a sale fare rather than paying the standing rate.

## Entry rules for UK travellers in 2026

- **No visa** for stays up to **90 days in any 180-day period** across the Schengen area.
- A **passport issued within the last 10 years** and **valid for at least 3 months** after your planned departure from Schengen — both enforced at the border.
- **ETIAS travel authorisation** once the scheme launches in 2026 — check the current status before you travel.
- UK travellers use the **e-gates** at Malaga where available.

Always check the [UK government's Spain travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/spain) shortly before you fly.

## Malaga Airport to the city and coast

Malaga-Costa del Sol Airport (AGP) has a **Cercanías C1 train** that reaches central Malaga in about **12 minutes** and runs along the coast towards Torremolinos, Benalmádena and Fuengirola. Frequent **buses** serve the city and the resorts, and taxis are plentiful. For central Malaga the train is fastest and cheapest; for resorts further along the Costa del Sol, check the bus or a pre-booked transfer. ## Common mistakes UK-Malaga travellers make

1. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Malaga flies from dozens of UK airports — your local one is almost always cheapest.
2. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £30 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can be beaten by a Jet2 fare that includes baggage for a beach week.
3. **Booking the summer peak late.** July-August is peak Costa del Sol — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
4. **Missing the May-June and September value window.** Great beach weather at well below peak-summer prices.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Overlooking Malaga city itself.** It's a genuinely good city break, not just an airport for the coast.
