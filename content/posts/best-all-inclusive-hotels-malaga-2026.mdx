## Málaga All-Inclusive FAQs

### Is Málaga a good base for a Costa del Sol beach holiday?

Yes — Málaga is the biggest city on the Costa del Sol and its natural gateway. It has its own city beaches (Malagueta and La Malagueta) plus the resort towns of Torremolinos, Benalmádena and Fuengirola all a short train or drive west along the C1 Cercanías line. That mix of a proper city break, culture and easy beach access is exactly why UK travellers keep choosing Málaga over a single-resort stay.

### Do Málaga hotels offer all-inclusive board?

A handful do — city hotels lean more towards room-only and bed-and-breakfast than the classic all-inclusive resort model. Several properties on this list, including SOL by Meliá Guadalmar and Futurotel Malagueta Beach, offer an all-inclusive option. For a fuller resort all-inclusive experience, base yourself in nearby Torremolinos or Benalmádena and use Málaga for day trips.

### How far is Málaga Airport (AGP) from the city centre?

Málaga–Costa del Sol Airport (AGP) sits about 8 km southwest of the city centre, roughly a 15-minute drive. The C1 Cercanías train runs from the airport to Málaga Centro-Alameda in around 12 minutes, and the same line continues to Torremolinos, Benalmádena and Fuengirola — making AGP one of the easiest airport-to-city hops in Spain.

### Is Málaga good for a city break as well as a beach holiday?

Very — it's one of Spain's best city-break-meets-beach destinations. The old town packs in the Picasso Museum, the Roman theatre, the Moorish Alcazaba fortress, the Gibralfaro castle and the Centre Pompidou, all walkable, then Malagueta beach sits minutes away. Few Spanish cities let you pair serious culture with a swim so easily.
