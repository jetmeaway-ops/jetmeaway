Fifteen minutes north is **Calis Beach**, a long shingle seafront famous across Britain for its sunsets and its relaxed café promenade. Over the mountain to the south is **Ölüdeniz**, home to the Blue Lagoon nature reserve and the beach where paragliders launched from Babadağ mountain drift down to land — Fethiye is the paragliding capital of Turkey. Between the two, up the hill, sit the lively resort villages of **Hisaronu and Ovacik**.

Because the protected Blue Lagoon limits beachfront building, many of Fethiye's all-inclusive resorts sit slightly inland in the pine hills with a shuttle to the sand, while the boutique end clusters in the harbour town. ## Beyond the Beach — Fethiye's Essentials

A few experiences worth planning around your stay:

- **Paraglide off Babadağ over the Blue Lagoon** — tandem flights launch from up to ~1,960m and glide down to land on Ölüdeniz beach. The signature Fethiye experience; book a calm morning slot.
- **12 Islands boat trip from the harbour** — a full-day cruise around the islands of Fethiye Bay with swim stops, leaving from the marina. The classic Fethiye day out.
- **Butterfly Valley by boat** — a dramatic gorge-beach reachable by boat from Ölüdeniz, home to the Jersey tiger butterfly and a waterfall walk inland.
- **Kayaköy ghost village** — an abandoned Greek hillside village of hundreds of roofless stone houses, a short trip from Fethiye, atmospheric at golden hour.
- **The Lycian rock tombs & Tomb of Amyntas** — cut into the cliff above Fethiye town, a 4th-century-BC Lycian burial façade you can climb up to for the harbour view.
- **Saklıkent Gorge** — an 18km canyon inland where you wade through cold mountain water between towering walls; pairs with the Roman ruins at Tlos.
- **Calis Beach sunset** — the north seafront is famous for its sunsets; a promenade of cafés and bars watches the sky over the bay each evening.

## UK Practicalities

- **Flights**: Dalaman (DLM) is the airport for Fethiye. TUI, Jet2, easyJet and other carriers run direct charter and scheduled flights from UK airports through the summer season. [Search flights to DLM](/flights?to=DLM).
- **Transfer**: DLM to Fethiye town is about 45-60 minutes by road; Ölüdeniz and Hisaronu are 15-20 minutes further. Most all-inclusive packages include the coach transfer.
- **Visa**: Not required for UK passport holders for stays under 90 days — just a passport valid for your stay; check the [UK Foreign Office Turkey advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/turkey) for the latest entry rules.
- **Currency**: Turkish lira (TRY). Room rates are quoted in EUR/USD/GBP; bring lira or a card for tips, boat trips, markets and taxis. Tipping 5-10% in restaurants, round up taxis.
- **Best months**: May, June, September and October (24-30°C, warm sea, full boat and paragliding season). July-August is hottest and busiest; April and late October are mild for sightseeing.
- **Budget**: Budget-tier trip — £64-160/night. Mid and luxury all-inclusive — £150-900/night. A 7-night budget stay built on this guide's town hotels can land comfortably under £700 per person before flights.
