## UK Practicalities

- **Airport & flights**: You fly into **Antalya (AYT)**, the gateway for the eastern Turkish Riviera. Heavy seasonal direct service from across the UK — Jet2, TUI, easyJet, Pegasus and others from London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow and more, roughly April–October. [Search flights to AYT](/flights?to=AYT).
- **Transfer**: Side is ~60–75km east of AYT, about a 50–75 minute drive. Pre-book a private or shared transfer, or take a taxi; coach transfers can take longer with multiple hotel drops.
- **Visa**: UK tourists currently don't need a visa for stays up to 90 days in 180 — confirm the latest government guidance before you travel.
- **Currency**: Turkish lira (TRY). At all-inclusive resorts you'll spend little day to day; carry some lira (euros and cards widely accepted) for tips, the dolmuş, ruins entry, the town and excursions.
- **Best months**: May, June, September and early October — hot, dry, warm sea, below the July–August peak. High summer is 33–38°C and busiest.
- **Tipping**: Not compulsory but normal — small tips to bar, restaurant and housekeeping staff are appreciated even on an all-inclusive board.
