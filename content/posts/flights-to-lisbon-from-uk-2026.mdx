## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Lisbon?** **Ryanair and easyJet** on headline price (from ~£22-£45 off-peak). **TAP Air Portugal** runs the most flights and usually includes a bag; **BA** is full-service.

**How long is the flight?** About **2 hours 40 to 2 hours 55 minutes** from the UK.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport on a budget carrier, but Lisbon flies direct from Manchester, Edinburgh and more — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer, Easter and the spring/autumn peaks.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & easyJet — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £22-£55 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. easyJet from Gatwick and beyond; Ryanair from Stansted and its regional bases. Ideal for a hand-luggage weekend.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra.

### TAP Air Portugal — the flag carrier

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£130 return; standard fares usually include a checked bag.
**UK airports:** Gatwick, Heathrow, Manchester and more.
**Pros:** Runs the most flights on the route (around 55 a week), a bag included on standard fares, and seamless onward connections within Portugal (Porto, the Algarve, Madeira) and to Brazil and Africa via its Lisbon hub.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the budget carriers; cheapest "Discount" fares are hand-luggage only, so check the fare type.

### British Airways — full-service

**Typical fare 2026:** £75-£140 return; cheapest "Basic" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** From Heathrow (around 29 flights a week), Avios earning, a bag on standard fares.
**Cons:** Pricier than the budget carriers.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair or easyJet win on price. Checking a bag or wanting onward connections within Portugal, TAP's standard fare is often better value than it looks. BA suits Avios collectors from Heathrow.

## UK airport comparison — fly local

Lisbon is served from a good spread of UK airports, so the cheapest option is often your **nearest** one. London (Gatwick, Stansted, Heathrow) has the most choice and the lowest budget fares, but Manchester and Edinburgh both have direct service on easyJet, Ryanair or TAP. For regional travellers, always price your local direct flight before assuming a cheap London fare wins once the London trek is added.

<a id="booking-windows"></a>

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Nov):** Book **4-8 weeks out**.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug) and Easter:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — fixed demand, fares only rise.
- **Spring/autumn peaks (Apr-Jun, Sep):** Book **6-10 weeks out** for Lisbon's best weather at lower prices.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

Set a price alert for your dates and book when a budget carrier drops a sale fare rather than paying the standing rate.

Lisbon's Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS) is close to the city. The **Metro (red line)** runs from the airport into the centre in about **20-30 minutes** for a couple of euros; the **Aerobus** and city buses also serve it; and a **taxi or ride-hail** takes 15-25 minutes for roughly €10-20. ## Common mistakes UK-Lisbon travellers make

1. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Lisbon flies direct from Manchester and Edinburgh too — your local one may be cheapest.
2. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £30 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can match a TAP fare that includes baggage.
3. **Booking the summer peak late.** July-August is peak — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
4. **Missing the spring/September value window.** Lisbon's best weather at below peak-summer prices.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Underestimating the hills.** Lisbon is steep — pack light and comfortable, another reason to travel hand-luggage only.
