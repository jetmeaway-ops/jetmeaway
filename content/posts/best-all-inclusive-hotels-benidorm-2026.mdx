## Benidorm All-Inclusive FAQs

### Which is the cheapest area for an all-inclusive hotel in Benidorm?

Benidorm splits between the busier Levante beach (bars, high-rises, nightlife) and the quieter, more family-friendly Poniente beach, plus the Old Town in between. Prices track hotel tier more than area — the cheapest bookable beds on our midweek searches start around £105 a night in the budget apartment tier, while the four-star all-inclusive hotels run from roughly £190 upward. For a real all-inclusive board, the value band is the £180–240 four-star tier.

### What's the difference between Levante and Poniente beach?

Levante is Benidorm's lively main beach — a wall of high-rise hotels, bars and the buzz most people picture. Poniente is the longer, calmer beach on the other side of the Old Town, favoured by families and couples who want a quieter base. Both are long, sandy, Blue Flag beaches; your choice is really about atmosphere.

### Which airport do I fly into for Benidorm?

Alicante Airport (ALC) is the gateway, with direct flights from most UK airports year-round and a heavy summer charter schedule. From ALC it's roughly a 45–60 minute transfer north up the Costa Blanca to Benidorm. [Search UK flights to ALC](/flights?to=ALC) and [compare live Benidorm hotel prices](/hotels?city=Benidorm) to line up your dates.

### Is Benidorm a year-round destination?

Yes — unlike many Spanish resorts, Benidorm stays busy all year thanks to its mild winter microclimate. Many hotels stay open through winter for the long-stay UK market, and the town keeps its bars, shows and restaurants running out of season. Summer is the beach-and-pool peak, but spring, autumn and winter breaks are all viable.
