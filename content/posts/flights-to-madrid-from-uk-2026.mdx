## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Madrid?** **Ryanair and easyJet** on headline price (from ~£18-£45 off-peak). **Iberia** runs the most flights and usually includes a bag; **BA and Air Europa** also fly.

**How long is the flight?** About **2 hours 27 minutes** from London, a little more from northern airports.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport on a budget carrier, but Madrid flies direct from Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Belfast — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer, Easter and big events.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & easyJet — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £18-£55 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. Ryanair from Stansted; easyJet from Gatwick, Luton and its regional bases. Ideal for a hand-luggage weekend.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra.

### Iberia — Spain's flag carrier

**Typical fare 2026:** £70-£130 return; standard fares usually include a checked bag.
**UK airports:** Heathrow and more, into Madrid (its home hub).
**Pros:** Runs the most flights on the route (around 76 a week), a bag included on standard fares, and seamless onward connections across Spain and to Latin America via its Madrid hub.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the budget carriers; cheapest fares are hand-luggage only, so check the fare type.

### British Airways & Air Europa

**Typical fare 2026:** BA £75-£140 return; Air Europa a mid-market option.
**Pros:** BA from Heathrow with Avios earning and a bag on standard fares; Air Europa adds capacity and onward Latin America connections.
**Cons:** BA's cheapest "Basic" fares are cabin-only.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair or easyJet win on price. Checking a bag or wanting onward Spanish connections, Iberia's standard fare is often better value than it looks. BA suits Avios collectors from Heathrow.

## UK airport comparison — fly local

Madrid is served from a good spread of UK airports, so the cheapest option is often your **nearest** one. London (Stansted, Gatwick, Luton, Heathrow) has the most choice, but Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Belfast all have direct service on Ryanair, easyJet, Iberia or BA. For regional travellers, always price your local direct flight before assuming a cheap London fare wins once the London trek is added.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Nov):** Book **4-8 weeks out**.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug) and Easter:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — fixed demand, fares only rise.
- **Spring/autumn peaks (Apr-Jun, Sep-Oct):** Book **6-10 weeks out** for Madrid's best weather at lower prices.
- **Big event dates (major football matches, concerts):** Book early — these spike hotels too.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

Set a price alert for your dates and book when a budget carrier drops a sale fare.

## Madrid Airport to the city centre

Madrid-Barajas Adolfo Suárez Airport (MAD) is well connected. The **Metro (line 8)** runs from the airport terminals into the city; the **Cercanías C-1 train** links Terminal 4 to central stations like Atocha and Chamartín in about **25-40 minutes**; and the **Airport Express bus** runs 24 hours to Atocha. A **taxi** has a fixed fare of around €30 to the centre. The Metro or train is the best value for most travellers. ## Common mistakes UK-Madrid travellers make

1. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Madrid flies direct from Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Belfast too — your local one may be cheapest.
2. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £30 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can match an Iberia fare that includes baggage.
3. **Booking mid-summer without expecting the heat.** Madrid is very hot in July-August; spring and autumn are more comfortable and cheaper.
4. **Booking over a big fixture by accident.** Major matches and concerts spike hotels — check the dates.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Overpacking for a weekend.** Madrid is a hand-luggage city break.
