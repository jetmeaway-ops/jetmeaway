**Where do I eat cheaply in Plovdiv?**
Plovdiv is a brilliant city for cheap, hearty eating. Traditional dishes like shopska salad, banitsa (cheese pastry), kavarma (slow-cooked meat) and grilled kebapche are filling and inexpensive, and Kapana and the main pedestrian street are packed with good-value restaurants, cafés, bakeries and craft-beer bars where a beer or a glass of Bulgarian wine costs a fraction of UK prices. Eating and drinking out here barely dents a budget.

**What is Kapana in Plovdiv?**
Kapana — meaning "the trap" for its maze of little streets — is Plovdiv's arty creative district right beside the Old Town, full of galleries, craft shops, street art, café terraces, craft-beer bars and restaurants. It is the buzzy heart of the city's food, drink and creative scene, and staying in or beside it puts you steps from the best of Plovdiv's nightlife and dining. Several guest houses and apartments in this guide sit in or on the edge of Kapana.
