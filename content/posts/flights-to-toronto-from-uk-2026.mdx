## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Toronto?** **Air Transat** direct from Gatwick and Manchester (from ~£300-£450 off-peak). **Air Canada and BA** are full-service from Heathrow; **WestJet** resumes Glasgow direct for summer 2026.

**How long is the flight?** About **8 hours** direct from London — one of the shorter transatlantic routes.

**Which UK airports fly direct?** **Heathrow** (Air Canada, BA), **Gatwick** (Air Transat), **Manchester** (Air Transat), **Glasgow** (WestJet, summer 2026).

**Do I need a visa?** No, but you **must have an approved eTA** (CAD 7, quick online) before flying.

**When should I book?** **3-5 months** ahead for summer, fall foliage and Christmas; **8-12 weeks** for winter/spring.

<a id="direct-vs-one-stop"></a>

## Direct vs one-stop

From **London and Glasgow**, direct is usually both faster and competitively priced given the carriers on the route — there's rarely a strong reason to connect. From **regional UK airports**, a one-stop routing via a European hub, or a domestic connection to a direct gateway, can be cheaper than positioning to London.

One important caveat: **connecting via a US airport requires a US ESTA even in transit**, because the US has no international transit zone — an extra step and cost. To keep it simple, choose a direct flight or a routing via a European hub. Compare the routing, not just the price.

## The Canada eTA you need (UK passport holders, 2026)

UK travellers flying to Canada need an approved **Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA)** before boarding:

- **Apply online** through the official Government of Canada website — approval is usually within minutes, but apply well ahead to be safe.
- The fee is a small **CAD 7**, and the eTA is valid for **up to five years** (or until your passport expires), covering multiple trips.
- **Avoid third-party sites** that charge a markup for what is a cheap official process.
- Your **passport must be valid** for the duration of your stay.

Always check the current rules on the [UK government's Canada travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/canada) before booking.

## Common mistakes UK-Toronto travellers make

1. **Forgetting the eTA.** You can't board without an approved eTA — apply online in advance (and use the official site).
2. **Connecting via the US without realising you need an ESTA.** US transit requires a US ESTA — choose a direct or via-Europe routing to avoid it.
3. **Judging fares on the headline price.** A cheap Air Transat or WestJet fare plus a checked bag can match an Air Canada or BA fare that includes one.
4. **Leaving a summer or fall-foliage booking late.** These peak fast — book 3-5 months ahead.
5. **Overlooking WestJet's new Glasgow direct.** For Scottish travellers it can beat connecting through London.
6. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online in advance on transatlantic routes.
7. **Missing the winter/spring value window.** Fares are lowest then, if you don't mind the cold.
