## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Athens?** **Ryanair and Wizz Air** on headline price (sometimes under £50 one-way shoulder-season). **BA** runs the most flights; **Aegean** is the full-service flag carrier; **easyJet and Jet2** also fly.

**How long is the flight?** About **3 hours 25 minutes to 4 hours** from London.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport on Ryanair or Wizz, but Athens flies direct from Manchester, Edinburgh, Birmingham and Bristol — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **6-10 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer and Easter.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & Wizz Air — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £50-£110 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares — sometimes under £50 one-way in shoulder season. Good frequency from London's budget airports.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra. On a near-4-hour flight, the comfort trade-off is more noticeable.

### British Airways — the frequency leader

**Typical fare 2026:** £120-£220 return; "Basic" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** The most flights on the route (around 39 a week), Avios earning, a bag on standard fares, schedule flexibility.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the budget carriers.

### Aegean — the Greek flag carrier

**Typical fare 2026:** £110-£200 return; standard fares include a bag.
**Pros:** Well-regarded full-service product, a checked bag and snack included, better legroom, and seamless domestic connections to the islands.
**Cons:** Pricier than the budget carriers on headline fare — though the gap narrows once you add a bag to a budget fare.

### easyJet & Jet2

**Typical fare 2026:** easyJet £70-£150; Jet2 with baggage included from the regions.
**Pros:** Useful frequencies and regional coverage; Jet2 for a baggage-included leisure option.
**Cons:** easyJet's cheapest fares are cabin-only.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair or Wizz win on price. For a bag, a fuller service or onward island flights, Aegean or BA are often better all-in value than they look. Jet2 suits regional beach-holiday travellers.

For regional travellers, always price your local direct flight before assuming a cheap London fare wins once the London trek is added.

<a id="islands"></a>

## Island-hopping from Athens

Athens is the main hub for the Greek islands, which is a big reason it's the most-searched Greece flight destination:

- **Domestic flights** — Aegean and Sky Express connect Athens to Santorini, Mykonos, Crete, Rhodes and more, often on the same ticket as your Aegean international flight.
- **Ferries** — the port of **Piraeus** (reachable by Metro line 3 from the airport and city) serves the Cyclades (Santorini, Mykonos, Naxos, Paros) and the Saronic islands.

Many travellers spend a day or two in Athens for the Acropolis and ancient sites, then continue to the islands — fly into Athens and plan the onward leg from there.

## Athens Airport to the city centre

Athens International Airport (ATH, Eleftherios Venizelos) is well connected. The **Metro (line 3)** runs from the airport to **Syntagma Square in about 40 minutes**, and the reliable **X95 express bus** runs 24 hours to the centre. A **taxi** has a fixed daytime fare to the city. Metro line 3 also continues to the port of Piraeus for island ferries. The Metro is the best value for most travellers. <a id="common-mistakes"></a>

## Common mistakes UK-Athens travellers make

1. **Sightseeing Athens in peak summer heat.** July-August often tops 35°C — spring and autumn are far more comfortable and cheaper.
2. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Athens flies direct from Manchester, Edinburgh and more — your local one may be cheapest.
3. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £60 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can approach an Aegean fare that includes one.
4. **Not planning the island leg.** If you're island-hopping, sort the domestic flight or Piraeus ferry before you go.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Forgetting Greek Orthodox Easter.** It's a major domestic travel period and can spike demand on different dates from Western Easter.
