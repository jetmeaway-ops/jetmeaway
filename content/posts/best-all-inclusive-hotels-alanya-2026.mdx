## UK Practicalities

- **Airports**: Gazipaşa-Alanya (GZP) is closest, 30–45 min transfer. Antalya (AYT) is larger with more UK flights but 120–130km west, a 2–2.5-hour transfer. [Search flights to GZP](/flights?to=GZP).
- **Visa**: None required for UK passport holders for tourist stays under 90 days. Check FCDO passport-validity guidance before travel.
- **Currency**: Turkish lira (TRY). Most all-inclusive room rates are quoted in EUR/USD/GBP, so bring lira or a card only for tips, taxis and off-resort spending.
- **Tipping**: Modest and appreciated — a few euros or lira for housekeeping and regular waiters; service is included in the all-inclusive board.
- **Best months**: May, June, September and October (24–30°C, warm sea, below-peak prices). July–August is hottest and dearest. Sea stays swimmable into October.
- **Budget**: Budget-tier week — from ~£230pp all-inclusive-adjacent on the cheapest beds. Mid-range 4-star — ~£730–1,260pp for the week. Luxury 5-star — from ~£840pp. Flights and transfers extra unless booked as a package.
