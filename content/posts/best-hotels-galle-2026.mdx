## What to See in Galle

**Galle Fort (UNESCO World Heritage).** The heart of any Galle trip — a Dutch colonial fort whose ramparts you can walk in a full circuit, with the old lighthouse, sea-wall sunsets, and a grid of historic streets packed with boutique cafés, galleries, jewellers and shops in restored colonial townhouses. Check local conditions before swimming, as currents can be strong outside the sheltered bays.

**The stilt fishermen.** An iconic south-coast sight — local fishers perched on thin wooden poles planted in the shallows, casting just offshore. They're seen along the coast near Galle, particularly toward Koggala and Weligama to the east. Ask before photographing, as many now expect a small tip.

## Galle Hotels FAQs

**Do UK travellers need a visa for Sri Lanka in 2026?**
Yes — UK passport holders need a Sri Lanka ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization) before arrival. Apply online at the official eta.gov.lk government site; it's usually approved quickly and covers tourist stays of up to 30 days. Only use the official government portal — third-party sites charge a mark-up for the same ETA.

**How do you get from Colombo airport to Galle?**
Galle sits about 120km south of Colombo's Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB). The Southern Expressway (E01) makes it roughly a 1.5 to 2 hour drive by private transfer or taxi. Pre-booking an airport transfer is simplest after a long-haul flight; shared shuttles and buses run the route more cheaply but take longer.

**What currency is used in Galle?**
The Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Cards are accepted at most hotels and larger restaurants inside Galle Fort, but carry cash for tuk-tuks, small cafés, market stalls and beach shacks. The pound figures in this guide are rough conversions only — always check the live LKR rate before you travel.

**Is there a train from Colombo to Galle?**
Yes — the coastal Southern Line runs from Colombo down to Galle and on to Matara, hugging the shoreline for much of the way. It's one of Sri Lanka's most scenic rail journeys, slower than the expressway but far more atmospheric. Trains are cheap; book a reserved seat in advance for busy periods.

**What is the best time to visit Galle?**
December to March is the south-coast dry season and the best time for Galle — warm, sunny and ideal for the beaches at Unawatuna and Jungle Beach.
