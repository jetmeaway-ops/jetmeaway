**What's the cheapest airline to Newcastle?** **easyJet** from Belfast and Bristol (from ~£25-£50 off-peak). **BA** from Heathrow; **Loganair** from Exeter and regional points.

**How long is the flight?** About **1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes** — ~3-4 hours door-to-door.

**Which UK cities fly?** Mainly **Belfast and Bristol (easyJet), London Heathrow (BA), Exeter (Loganair)** — with more coming as easyJet opens its Newcastle base in March 2026.

<a id="fly-vs-train"></a>

## Fly or take the train to Newcastle?

Newcastle is on the fast East Coast Main Line, so the mode depends on your origin:

- **From London:** the **LNER train** (about 3 hours city centre to city centre) is often the better choice — flying is only via Heathrow on BA, and door-to-door the train usually wins once airport transfers are added.
- **From Yorkshire, Edinburgh and the East Coast:** the train is fast and frequent — usually quickest.
- **From Belfast (a sea crossing) and the far South West:** fly — the rail journey is very long or crosses water.

Match the mode to your city: rail for the East Coast, air for Belfast and the South West.

## Do you need ID or a passport?

Newcastle is in the UK, so a flight from elsewhere in Britain is **domestic** — no passport is legally required. But **every airline requires an accepted form of photo ID**, and each sets its own list. Most accept a valid passport, and some accept a photo driving licence on domestic routes. **Check your airline's accepted-ID list and take an ID it accepts.**

## Common mistakes UK-Newcastle travellers make

1. **Flying London-Newcastle instead of taking the train.** The LNER (~3 hours) usually beats flying via Heathrow — check it first.
2. **Judging fares on the headline price.** Add airport transfers and a hold bag and a "cheap" flight can cost more.
3. **Travelling with no accepted photo ID.** It's domestic, but airlines require ID — take one they accept.
4. **Missing easyJet's new-base sales.** New routes from March 2026 often launch with competitive fares.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online, or travel hand-luggage only.
6. **Ignoring event dates.** Concerts and matches spike hotels — check the calendar.
7. **Overlooking Northumberland.** Newcastle is the gateway to Hadrian's Wall and the coast — plan a day out.
