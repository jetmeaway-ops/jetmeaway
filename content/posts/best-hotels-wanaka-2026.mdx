**How far is Wānaka from Queenstown?**
About an hour's drive, roughly 70 km via the Crown Range Road, the scenic alpine pass that's New Zealand's highest sealed public road. An alternative valley route via Cromwell takes a little longer but avoids the mountain pass, which is useful in icy winter conditions. The two towns are an easy pairing for a South Island trip.

**Is Wānaka expensive?**
