## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### TUI — the value and package leader

**The honest read:** for a bundled all-inclusive beach holiday, TUI is often the best value. For a scheduled flight-only fare with a bag and Avios, BA from Gatwick. Compare a package quote against flight-only plus a hotel for your exact dates and hotel.

<a id="direct-vs-connecting"></a>

## Direct vs connecting

From **London and the TUI regional airports**, direct is usually both faster and competitively priced, and it avoids the complication of a connection on a long-haul leisure trip. A one-stop routing can occasionally be cheaper from a regional airport, but **connecting via a US airport requires a US ESTA even in transit** — an extra step and cost. For most UK travellers, a direct flight or a package is the simplest and best value.

## Hurricane season — timing and travel insurance

The **Atlantic hurricane season runs roughly June to November, peaking in September and October**. Direct hits on Cancun are relatively infrequent, and many travellers visit in this window without issue — but it brings more rain, humidity and a small storm risk, which is exactly why fares are lower.

If you travel in hurricane season:

- **Take comprehensive travel insurance** covering cancellation and disruption — it's strongly recommended anyway on a long-haul trip.
- **Build in flexibility** where you can, and keep an eye on forecasts close to travel.
- **The trade-off is real value** — summer and early-autumn fares are the cheapest of the year.

For guaranteed sunshine, travel December-April and book early; for the lowest price, travel in the shoulder/hurricane window with good insurance.

## Common mistakes UK-Cancun travellers make

1. **Not comparing flight-only against a package.** Both can win depending on dates and hotel — always check both.
2. **Booking the winter-sun peak late.** December-April, especially Christmas and half-term, spike fast — book 3-5 months ahead.
3. **Connecting via the US without realising you need an ESTA.** US transit requires a US ESTA — a direct flight or package avoids it.
4. **Skipping travel insurance in hurricane season.** June-November brings storm and disruption risk — insure comprehensively.
5. **Comparing a BA "Basic" fare to a bag-included fare.** Basic has no checked bag — compare the all-in cost for a beach trip.
6. **Booking a transfer without checking resort distance.** Playa del Carmen and Tulum are an hour or more south of the airport.
7. **Leaving a passport with under six months' validity.** Mexico wants six months from departure — check before booking.
