## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Milan?** **Ryanair, easyJet and Wizz Air** — the lowest headline fares (from ~£16-£45 off-peak). **easyJet** runs the most flights; **BA and ITA** are full-service.

**How long is the flight?** About **2 hours** from London.

**Which airport?** **Linate (LIN)** is closest to the centre (15-min metro); **Malpensa (MXP)** is the biggest (50-min train); **Bergamo (BGY)** is furthest (budget carriers, ~1hr coach).

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually a **London** airport on a budget carrier, but Milan flies direct from Manchester, Edinburgh and more — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **4-8 weeks** ahead most months; **8-12 weeks** for summer, fashion/design weeks and ski season.

**Do I need a visa?** No — **90 days visa-free** in Schengen. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

## Which Milan airport should you fly into?

Milan has three airports at very different distances from the city — this is the key decision on the route.

| Airport | Distance to centre | Transfer | Airlines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linate (LIN) | ~7km E | M4 metro, ~15 min | ITA, easyJet |
| Malpensa (MXP) | ~50km NW | Malpensa Express train, ~50 min | easyJet, Ryanair, Wizz, BA |
| Bergamo (BGY) | ~45-50km NE | Coach, ~1 hour | Ryanair, Wizz |

**The convenience call.** **Linate** is far and away the most convenient — a 15-minute metro ride into central Milan — so for a short city break it's worth prioritising if the fare is close. **Malpensa** is the biggest with the most flights and a reliable express train. **Bergamo** (marketed as "Milan Bergamo") is a genuine budget gateway but around an hour away by coach, so a Bergamo fare should be clearly cheaper than a Linate or Malpensa one to be worth the longer transfer. Check the all-in cost and transfer time, not just the headline fare.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair, easyJet & Wizz Air — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £16-£50 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares. easyJet runs the most flights into Malpensa (around 53 a week) and crucially also serves the central **Linate**; Ryanair and Wizz use Malpensa and Bergamo. Ideal for a hand-luggage weekend.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; extras cost more. Ryanair and Wizz often use the further-out Bergamo — check the airport.

### British Airways & ITA Airways — full-service

**Typical fare 2026:** £75-£150 return; cheapest "Basic"/"Light" fares are cabin-only.
**Pros:** BA from Heathrow, ITA into the central Linate (its focus), Avios earning (BA), a bag on standard fares, onward connections within Italy (ITA).
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the budget carriers.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only, Ryanair, easyJet or Wizz win on price — but check which airport, as Bergamo adds transfer time. For the central Linate and a bag included, easyJet (Linate), ITA or BA. Compare the all-in price and factor the airport.

## UK airport comparison — fly local

Milan is served from a good spread of UK airports, so the cheapest option is often your **nearest** one. London (Stansted, Luton, Gatwick, Heathrow) has the most choice across all carriers, but Manchester, Edinburgh, Birmingham and Bristol also have direct service, mostly on easyJet. For regional travellers, always price your local direct flight before assuming a cheap London fare wins once the London trek is added.

## Common mistakes UK-Milan travellers make

1. **Booking a Bergamo fare thinking it's central Milan.** Bergamo is ~1 hour away by coach — factor the transfer.
2. **Overlooking Linate.** It's a 15-minute metro from the centre; for a short trip it's worth a slightly higher fare.
3. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £30 Bergamo fare plus a bag and coach can match an easyJet fare into central Linate.
4. **Booking over Fashion Week or Salone del Mobile by accident.** These spike prices hard — check the dates.
5. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
6. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
7. **Forgetting Milan's ski and lakes access.** Ski weekends spike fares; if that's your plan, book early.
