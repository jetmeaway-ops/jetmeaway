## Quick answers

**What's the cheapest airline to Geneva?** **easyJet** — the lowest headline fares (from ~£29-£60 off-peak). **BA and SWISS** are full-service with a bag included; **Jet2** is ski-friendly from the regions.

**How long is the flight?** About **1 hour 30 to 1 hour 50 minutes** from London — quick for a ski weekend.

**Which UK airport is cheapest?** Usually **Gatwick** on easyJet, but Geneva flies direct from Edinburgh, Glasgow, Aberdeen, Belfast, Newcastle and Leeds too, especially in ski season — flying **local** is often cheapest.

**When should I book?** **3-4 months** ahead for ski season (especially Christmas, New Year, February half-term); **6-8 weeks** for summer and shoulder.

**Do I need a visa?** No — Switzerland is in **Schengen**, so **90 days visa-free**. Check ETIAS status before you travel.

**Money?** Switzerland uses the **Swiss franc, not the euro** — pay by card in francs; it's an expensive country.

## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### easyJet — the price leader

**Typical fare 2026:** £29-£80 return off-peak, hand-luggage only; much more on ski weekends.
**UK airports:** Gatwick with the most frequency, plus regional bases including strong ski-season routes.
**Pros:** The lowest headline fares and the widest budget frequency to Geneva. Ideal for a hand-luggage ski or city weekend.
**Cons:** Hold bag, allocated seat and ski/snowboard carriage all cost extra — the fare climbs once you add ski gear.

### British Airways & SWISS — full-service

**Typical fare 2026:** £90-£200 return; cheapest fares may be cabin-only.
**Pros:** A checked bag on standard fares, better legroom, and ski equipment carried (check the fare). SWISS offers seamless onward Swiss connections.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than easyJet.

### Jet2 — the ski-friendly regional option

**Typical fare 2026:** £80-£180 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance and ski carriage.
**Pros:** Baggage often included, ski-friendly service, strong regional coverage — frequently the best all-in value for a ski trip with equipment from outside London.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than easyJet; fewer London departures.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only for a city break, easyJet wins. For a ski trip with a hold bag and skis, Jet2's all-in fare (or BA/SWISS with baggage) is often better value once you add carriage to an easyJet fare. Always compare the all-in cost with ski gear.

## Skiing from Geneva — French and Swiss Alps

Geneva is the UK's premier ski-gateway flight because its airport has both Swiss and French sectors, opening up resorts on both sides of the border:

- **French Alps** — Chamonix (about an hour), Morzine, Les Gets, Avoriaz, La Clusaz, Flaine — mostly 1-2 hours by transfer coach or hire car.
- **Swiss Alps** — Verbier, Villars, Les Diablerets and more, reachable by train or road.

Resort transfer coaches, hire cars and trains all connect from the airport. If you're skiing, book a ski-friendly airline (Jet2, BA, SWISS, or easyJet with paid ski carriage) and add your equipment at booking.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Ski season (Dec-Mar):** Book **3-4 months ahead** — especially for Christmas, New Year, February half-term and any Saturday, the most in-demand slots.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug):** Book **6-10 weeks ahead**.
- **Shoulder months (Apr-Jun, Sep-Oct):** Book **6-8 weeks ahead** — the cheapest and best-value window if you're not skiing.
- **Ski weekends:** Saturday flights are the priciest — a midweek or Sunday flight can save a lot.

Set a price alert for your dates and book early for any winter-weekend trip.

## Entry rules and money for UK travellers in 2026

- **No visa** — Switzerland is in the **Schengen area** (though not the EU), so UK travellers get **90 days visa-free** in any 180-day period.
- A **passport issued within the last 10 years** and **valid for at least 3 months** after departure — both enforced.

## Common mistakes UK-Geneva travellers make

1. **Booking a ski weekend late.** Christmas, New Year, February half-term and Saturdays are the priciest slots — book 3-4 months ahead.
2. **Adding ski carriage at the airport.** Always book skis/snowboard online in advance — far cheaper and guarantees space.
3. **Assuming Switzerland uses the euro.** It's the franc — pay by card in francs and decline "pay in pounds."
4. **Under-budgeting for the resort.** Switzerland (and the flight) are expensive; the ski trip cost dwarfs the fare.
5. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Geneva flies direct from many UK airports in ski season — your local one may be cheapest and ski-friendlier.
6. **Missing the shoulder-season value.** Spring and autumn are the cheapest and lovely for lakes and hiking.
7. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule applies to Switzerland too.
