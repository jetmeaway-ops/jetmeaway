## What to do in Kenting

Kenting is Taiwan's southern beach resort, tucked inside **Kenting National Park** at the island's tropical tip. The days here are built around the coast: calm **swimming coves** and livelier **surf beaches** along the peninsula, **coral reefs and snorkelling** in the warm water, and quieter stretches to walk. Head to the **Eluanbi lighthouse and cape** at Taiwan's southernmost point for the classic view where two seas meet, then wander the park's forest and grassland trails inland. After dark, the **Kenting Night Market** fills the main strip with Taiwanese street food, drinks and stalls — the cheap, easy way to eat well. It stays warm year-round, but keep an eye on the **typhoon season (roughly June to September)** if you're travelling in summer.

**What is the Kenting Night Market?**
It is the lively evening street market along the main Kenting strip, packed with Taiwanese street-food stalls, drinks, snacks and shops. It is one of the area's signature after-dark activities and an easy, cheap way to eat well. Most of the hotels in this guide are a short trip from the action.

**Is English spoken in Kenting?**
Mandarin Chinese is the local language. English is more limited than in Taipei but you will find some at the larger resorts and tourist-facing businesses, and signage at major sights often includes English. A translation app and a few basic phrases go a long way; Taiwanese hospitality is famously warm and helpful.

**Do I need a car in Kenting?**
A car or scooter helps you reach spread-out beaches, the lighthouse and quieter coves at your own pace, and many visitors rent a scooter locally. That said, the Kenting Street area, the night market and the nearest beaches are walkable, and the Taiwan Tourist Shuttle links the main sights if you would rather not drive.
