## Airline-by-airline comparison (2026)

### Ryanair & Wizz Air — the price leaders

**Typical fare 2026:** £18-£50 return off-peak, hand-luggage only.
**Pros:** Ryanair dominates the route (around 63% of London-Krakow bookings) with the lowest headline fares; Wizz Air (around 27%) is a strong Central-Europe specialist. High frequency from London's budget airports.
**Cons:** Cheapest fares are cabin-only; a larger cabin bag, hold bag and allocated seat all cost extra.

### Jet2 — the regional, baggage-included option

**Typical fare 2026:** £60-£120 return, usually with a generous baggage allowance.
**UK airports:** Glasgow, Birmingham, Manchester, Newcastle, Leeds Bradford, Liverpool, East Midlands.
**Pros:** Baggage often included, friendly service, and the widest regional UK coverage on the route — frequently the best all-in value if you're flying from outside London or checking a bag.
**Cons:** Higher headline fare than the ultra-budget carriers.

### easyJet

**Typical fare 2026:** £40-£90 return, hand-luggage only by default.
**Pros:** Adds frequency and regular sales.
**Cons:** Hold bag and allocated seat cost extra.

**The honest read:** hand-luggage only from London, Ryanair or Wizz win on price. From a regional airport or checking a bag, Jet2's all-in fare and coverage are hard to beat. For a weekend, travel cabin-only and the budget fare stays tiny.

## Money in Krakow: zloty, not euro

A quick but useful point: **Poland keeps its own currency, the Polish zloty (PLN)** — it is not in the euro despite being in the EU and Schengen.

- **Krakow is very good value** for UK visitors — food, drink and going out cost far less than at home, which is part of the appeal.
- **Card payment is widely accepted**; when a machine offers to **charge you in pounds instead of zloty, always choose zloty** (declining dynamic currency conversion avoids a hidden markup).
- **Avoid airport currency exchange**, where rates are worst — withdraw zloty from a reputable bank ATM in the city or pay by card.

## The booking windows that actually save money

- **Off-peak months (Jan, Feb, Oct, Nov):** Book **around 6 weeks out** — October is often the cheapest month.
- **Summer (Jul-Aug), Christmas markets, New Year:** Book **8-12 weeks out** — fixed demand, fares only rise.
- **Weekend breaks:** A Thursday or Saturday departure often undercuts the Friday-evening rush.

Set a price alert for your dates and book when a budget carrier drops a sale fare.

## Common mistakes UK-Krakow travellers make

1. **Assuming Poland uses the euro.** It's the zloty — pay by card in zloty and decline "pay in pounds."
2. **Routing through London unnecessarily.** Jet2 flies Krakow from many regional airports — your local one may be cheapest.
3. **Judging budget fares on the headline price.** A £25 Ryanair fare plus a £45 hold bag can be beaten by a Jet2 fare that includes baggage.
4. **Missing October.** It's often the single cheapest month to fly the route.
5. **Booking the Christmas markets late.** December is Krakow's peak — book 8-12 weeks ahead.
6. **Adding baggage at the airport.** Always add it online at booking.
7. **Assuming an old passport is fine.** The Schengen 10-year issue rule catches UK travellers out.
