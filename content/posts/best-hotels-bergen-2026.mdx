Bergen is the gateway to the fjords, so much of the joy is using it as a base for the water and mountains around it.

**Which Bergen hotels are near the cruise terminal?**
Bergen's main cruise ships dock at the Skolten and Bontelabo terminals just north of Bryggen, and at the Jekteviken/Dokken terminal to the southwest. The central hotels around Bryggen and the Fish Market — Radisson Blu Royal Hotel, Scandic Torget Bergen, Grand Hotel Terminus and the Thon hotels — are all within walking distance of the Skolten quay. If you are catching a fjord cruise rather than an ocean liner, those leave from the Strandkaiterminalen by the Fish Market, right in the centre and close to most of the hotels in this guide.
